## This Week's Goals

- Learn vocabulary for comedy expressions and reactions in manga
- Understand terms for funny situations and mishaps
- Master comedic sound effects used in Japanese manga

## Grammar Focus

**Comedic Expression Constructions:** Using onomatopoeia and mimetic words for comedic effect

**Rule:** Japanese humor often uses onomatopoeia (words that imitate sounds) and mimetic words (words that describe states or actions) to enhance comedic moments

Examples:
- **フフン (fufun)** - sound of snickering
- **クスクス (kusukusu)** - sound of giggling/chuckling
- **ぷっ (puff)** - sound of suppressed laughter

This lesson continues our vocabulary-focused approach by emphasizing humor terms essential for understanding comedic moments in manga.

- **You're kidding** - 冗談でしょ? (jōdan desho?)** - Are you kidding?
