## Grammar Focus

**Dramatic Expression Constructions:** Using intensifiers and emotional markers in dramatic scenes

**Rule:** Japanese dramatic expressions often use intensifiers and emotional markers to emphasize the gravity of the situation

Examples:
- **本当に (ほんとうに)** - really/truly (intensifier)
- **一体 (いったい)** - on earth (for emphasis in drama)
- **どうやって (どうやって)** - how (in disbelief)
- **なぜ (なぜ)** - why (emphatic in dramatic scenes)

This lesson continues our vocabulary-focused approach by emphasizing terms essential for understanding dramatic and conflict-laden scenes in manga.

### Dramatic Dialogue Expressions
