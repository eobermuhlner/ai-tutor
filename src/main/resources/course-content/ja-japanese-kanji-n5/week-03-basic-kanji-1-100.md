## This Week's Goals

- Learn kanji related to school and education
- Master work and occupation terms
- Understand transportation vocabulary
- Practice weather and seasonal expressions

## Grammar Focus

**Minimal grammar**: Focus on recognizing and reading kanji rather than complex grammatical structures.

**Basic expressions**:
- **School**: **学校 (がっこう)**, **学生 (がくせい)**, **先生 (せんせい)**
- **Work**: **会社 (かいしゃ)**, **仕事 (しごと)**, **働く (はたらく)**

Examples:
- **学校に行きます** - Gakkou ni ikimasu (I go to school)
- **会社で働きます** - Kaisha de hatarakimasu (I work at the company)

## Conversation Scenarios

Practice using kanji related to education, work, transportation, and weather. The tutor will help you use these characters in context.

Topics to cover:
- Discussing school and study habits
- Talking about jobs and work responsibilities
- Asking for directions and transportation information
- Describing weather and seasonal changes

Tutor will prompt: "Can you describe your school life with these kanji?" or "How do you commute to work?"

## Practice Patterns

1. Write each kanji 3 times with correct stroke order
2. Create sentences about school and education
3. Practice describing jobs and work
4. Use transportation vocabulary in context
5. Express weather and seasonal information
6. Practice reading simple phrases with the new characters

## Common Mistakes to Watch

- **Confusing similar kanji** → 学 (study) vs 校 (school) - different meanings
- **Using wrong readings** → 学生 (がくせい) vs 先生 (せんせい) - different readings
- **Forgetting context for readings** → Some kanji change meaning in compounds
- **Mixing up work terms** → 仕事 (job) vs 会社 (company) - different concepts
- **Stroke order errors** → Education kanji often have complex strokes

## Cultural Notes

- Education is highly valued in Japanese society
- Work culture emphasizes dedication and teamwork
- Public transportation is extremely efficient and punctual in Japan
- Weather and seasons are deeply integrated into Japanese culture and art
- The concept of "kigo" (seasonal words) is important in Japanese poetry
- Japan has four distinct seasons with specific cultural associations
