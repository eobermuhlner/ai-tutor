## Lesson Goals

- Learn kanji related to technology and communication
- Master vocabulary for science and mathematics
- Understand abstract concept terms
- Practice advanced vocabulary

## Grammar Focus

**Minimal grammar**: Focus on recognizing and reading kanji rather than complex grammatical structures.

**Review expressions**:
- **Technology**: **技術 (ぎじゅつ)**, **通信 (つうしん)**, **コンピューター**
- **Math**: **数学 (すうがく)**, **計算 (けいさん)**, **数字 (すうじ)**

Examples:
- **技術を学びます** - Gijutsu wo manabimasu (I learn technology)
- **計算をします** - Keisan wo shimasu (I calculate)

## Conversation Scenarios

Practice using kanji related to technology, science, and abstract concepts. The tutor will help you use these characters in context.

Topics to cover:
- Discussing technology and communication
- Talking about scientific concepts
- Expressing abstract ideas
- Describing mathematical concepts

Tutor will prompt: "Can you explain this scientific concept?" or "How would you use this technology term?"

## Practice Patterns

1. Write each kanji 3 times with correct stroke order
2. Create complex sentences using multiple kanji
3. Practice reading longer phrases with many kanji
4. Use all learned vocabulary in meaningful contexts
5. Connect different categories of kanji together
6. Practice reading comprehension with mixed kanji and kana

## Common Mistakes to Watch

- **Confusing similar kanji** → 計 (plan) vs 算 (calculate) - different meanings
- **Using wrong readings** → 計算 (けいさん) vs 数字 (すうじ) - different readings
- **Forgetting context for readings** → Some kanji change meaning in compounds
- **Mixing up abstract concepts** → 抽象 (abstract) vs 具体 (concrete) - opposite meanings
- **Stroke order errors** → Technology kanji often have complex strokes

## Cultural Notes

- Japan is known for its technological innovations and high-tech society
- Mathematical concepts in Japan often use both native Japanese and Chinese-derived readings
- Abstract thinking and philosophical concepts have deep roots in Japanese culture
- The balance between traditional and modern elements is important in Japanese society
- Science and technology education is highly valued in Japan
- Japan has contributed significantly to global technological advancement
