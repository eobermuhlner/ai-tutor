## Lesson Goals

- Make appointments at hair salons or barbershops
- Describe desired haircut or style
- Understand and request salon services
- Communicate about hair length and preferences
- Handle payment and tipping

## Grammar Focus

### Describing Desired Changes

**Rule:** Use "Quiero..." (I want...) + infinitive or noun to describe what you want done.

Examples:
- "Quiero cortarme el pelo" (I want to cut my hair)
- "Quiero un corte" (I want a haircut)
- "Solo las puntas" (Just the ends)

### Describing Length

**Rule:** Use measurements or comparative terms: "Dos centímetros" (Two centimeters), "Más corto" (Shorter), "Así de largo" (This long).

Examples:
- "Cortar dos centímetros" (Cut two centimeters)
- "Más corto aquí" (Shorter here)
- "Así" (Like this - pointing)

## Conversation Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Making an Appointment

Practice calling or visiting salon to make appointment. Specify date, time, and service needed.

Essential phrases:
- "Quiero pedir cita" (I want to make an appointment)
- "¿Tiene hora para mañana?" (Do you have time tomorrow?)
- "Para cortar el pelo" (For a haircut)
- "Por la tarde, si es posible" (In the afternoon, if possible)

### Scenario 2: Describing Your Desired Haircut

Practice explaining what haircut you want. Use photos, gestures, and descriptions.

Essential phrases:
- "Quiero cortarme el pelo" (I want to cut my hair)
- "Solo las puntas, por favor" (Just the ends, please)
- "Dos centímetros" (Two centimeters)
- "No muy corto" (Not very short)
- "Así" (Like this - show with fingers)
- "Como esta foto" (Like this photo)

### Scenario 3: During the Haircut

Practice communicating during the service. Give feedback and make adjustments.

Essential phrases:
- "Un poco más corto aquí" (A little shorter here)
- "Está bien así" (It's good like this)
- "Los lados más cortos" (Sides shorter)
- "No tocar el flequillo" (Don't touch the bangs)

### Scenario 4: Additional Services

Practice requesting additional services beyond basic haircut. Ask about prices and options.

Essential phrases:
- "¿Cuánto cuesta el lavado?" (How much does the wash cost?)
- "¿Hacen barba también?" (Do you do beards too?)
- "Quiero arreglar la barba" (I want to trim my beard)
- "¿Cuánto cuesta en total?" (How much in total?)

## Practice Patterns

- Successfully make salon appointment for convenient time
- Describe desired haircut using Spanish measurements
- Give feedback during haircut to get desired result
- Request additional services and understand pricing
- Complete transaction including appropriate tip

## Common Mistakes to Watch

- "Cortar mi cabello" → "Cortarme el pelo" (use reflexive form)
- Saying "muy corto" when you mean "más corto" → "Muy" = very/too, "más" = more/shorter
- Not bringing photo → Pictures worth thousand words! Show photo of desired style
- "Solo un poco" without specifying → Be specific! "Un centímetro", "Dos dedos", show with fingers
- Forgetting reflexive → "Quiero cortar el pelo" (I want to cut hair) vs "Quiero cortarme el pelo" (I want to cut my hair)
- Not clarifying scissors vs clippers → "Con tijeras" or "con máquina" - specify if you care

## Cultural Notes

- **Appointments recommended**: Walk-ins possible but appointments ("citas") preferred, especially weekends. Call ahead or book online.
- **Prices vary widely**: Basic men's cut 10-15 euros at barbershop, 20-30 at salon. Women's cut/style 30-50 euros minimum. Color/highlights expensive (60-150 euros).
- **Wash included**: Most haircuts include wash. If not, they'll ask "¿Con lavado?" (With wash?).
- **Tipping expected**: Tip 5-10% or round up. Not mandatory but standard for good service. Leave cash even if paying by card.
- **Unisex salons common**: Most "peluquerías" serve everyone. "Barberías" specifically for men, traditional barbershops.
- **Show a photo**: Language barrier for haircuts can be tough. Always show photo of desired style on your phone.
- **They may be chatty**: Spanish hairdressers often very friendly and chatty. Good Spanish practice! Feel free to chat or say "Estoy cansado/a" (I'm tired) if you prefer quiet.
- **Blow-dry standard for women**: Women's haircuts usually include styling/blow-dry. Men usually just cut, no styling.
- **Metric measurements**: Spain uses centimeters, not inches. 2-3 cm = about 1 inch.
- **Siesta hours**: Some salons close 2-5pm. Check hours. Many open late (until 8-9pm) to compensate.
