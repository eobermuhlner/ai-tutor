## This Week's Goals

- Shop for souvenirs, clothes, and food at stores and markets
- Ask about prices, sizes, and availability
- Try on clothes and make purchasing decisions
- Negotiate prices at markets when appropriate
- Handle payment and returns

## Grammar Focus

### Asking About Prices

**Rule:** Use "¿Cuánto cuesta?" (How much does it cost?) for singular items, "¿Cuánto cuestan?" for plural.

Examples:
- "¿Cuánto cuesta esto?" (How much does this cost?)
- "¿Cuánto cuestan las naranjas?" (How much do the oranges cost?)
- "¿A cuánto está el kilo?" (How much per kilo?)

### Demonstratives for Pointing

**Rule:** Use "este/esta" (this), "ese/esa" (that), "aquel/aquella" (that over there) when pointing.

Examples:
- "Quiero este" (I want this one)
- "¿Puedo ver esa camisa?" (Can I see that shirt?)
- "Aquel libro" (That book over there)

## Conversation Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Shopping for Clothes

Practice shopping in a clothing store. Ask about sizes, colors, and try on items before deciding to purchase.

Essential phrases:
- "Busco una camiseta" (I'm looking for a t-shirt)
- "¿Tiene en talla 38?" (Do you have in size 38?)
- "¿En azul?" (In blue?)
- "¿Puedo probarme esto?" (Can I try this on?)
- "¿Dónde está el probador?" (Where is the fitting room?)
- "Me lo llevo" (I'll take it)

### Scenario 2: Buying Fresh Food at a Market

Practice shopping at a fruit and vegetable market. Ask about prices per kilo, request specific quantities, and select fresh produce.

Essential phrases:
- "¿A cuánto está el kilo de naranjas?" (How much per kilo of oranges?)
- "Medio kilo, por favor" (Half a kilo, please)
- "¿Están frescas?" (Are they fresh?)
- "Las más maduras" (The ripest ones)
- "¿Cuánto es en total?" (How much is it in total?)

### Scenario 3: Buying Souvenirs

Practice shopping for souvenirs and gifts. Ask about prices, compare items, and make decisions.

Essential phrases:
- "¿Cuánto cuesta este?" (How much does this one cost?)
- "¿Tiene algo más barato?" (Do you have something cheaper?)
- "¿Cuánto por dos?" (How much for two?)
- "Me llevo este y ese" (I'll take this one and that one)

### Scenario 4: Returning or Exchanging an Item

Practice returning or exchanging a purchase. Explain the problem and request a refund or exchange.

Essential phrases:
- "Quiero devolver esto" (I want to return this)
- "Me queda muy grande" (It's too big for me)
- "¿Puedo cambiarlo?" (Can I exchange it?)
- "Aquí está el recibo" (Here is the receipt)
- "¿Puedo probarlo de nuevo?" (Can I try it again?)

## Practice Patterns

- Successfully purchase clothing after trying on and selecting size
- Buy fresh produce at a market using weight-based pricing
- Compare multiple items and make a purchasing decision
- Negotiate a better price at an appropriate market setting
- Return or exchange an item using the receipt

## Common Mistakes to Watch

- "¿Cuánto es esto?" → "¿Cuánto cuesta esto?" (use "cuesta" for asking price of items)
- Confusing "talla" (clothing size) with "número" (shoe size) → Use correct term!
- "Dónde son los probadores" → "Dónde están los probadores" (use "están" for locations)
- Bargaining in regular stores → Only negotiate at markets, not fixed-price stores
- Not asking "¿Puedo devolver?" before buying → Return policies vary, ask first if uncertain
- Forgetting to say "por favor" → Politeness essential in all transactions

## Cultural Notes

- **Fixed prices vs bargaining**: Stores have fixed prices (no bargaining). Street markets and some vendor stalls allow negotiation, but be respectful.
- **Siesta hours**: Many small shops close 2-5pm for siesta. Chain stores and malls stay open. Plan shopping accordingly.
- **Trying on clothes**: Always ask "¿Puedo probarme esto?" before using fitting room. Taking items without asking is rude.
- **Bring your own bags**: Plastic bags cost extra in Spain. Bring reusable bag or pay for bags.
- **Euro coins essential**: Markets and small shops prefer cash. Have coins for small purchases.
- **VAT (IVA) refunds**: Non-EU tourists can get VAT refunds on purchases over certain amount. Ask for "factura para tax-free" at time of purchase.
- **Polite browsing**: "Estoy mirando" (I'm just looking) is perfectly acceptable. No pressure to buy.
- **Sunday closures**: Most shops except malls closed on Sundays. Some tourist areas have exceptions.
- **Market etiquette**: Don't touch produce without asking. Vendors select items for you. Point and say "esas naranjas" (those oranges).
- **Receipt always**: Get receipt ("el recibo"). Needed for returns and legally required in Spain.
