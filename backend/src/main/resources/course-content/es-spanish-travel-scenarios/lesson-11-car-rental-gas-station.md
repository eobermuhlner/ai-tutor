## Lesson Goals

- Rent a car and understand insurance and rental terms
- Fill up with gas and pay at gas stations
- Ask for directions while driving
- Handle basic car problems
- Navigate parking situations

## Grammar Focus

### Asking for Services

**Rule:** Use "Necesito..." (I need...) or "Quiero..." (I want...) to request car-related services.

Examples:
- "Necesito alquilar un coche" (I need to rent a car)
- "Quiero llenar el depósito" (I want to fill the tank)
- "Necesito ayuda" (I need help)

### Giving and Receiving Directions While Driving

**Rule:** Use imperative forms for directions: "Gire" (Turn), "Siga" (Continue), "Tome" (Take).

Examples:
- "Gire a la derecha" (Turn right)
- "Siga todo recto" (Continue straight)
- "Tome la segunda salida" (Take the second exit)

## Conversation Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Renting a Car

Practice renting a car at the agency. Ask about vehicle types, insurance options, pricing, and pickup/return procedures.

Essential phrases:
- "Quiero alquilar un coche por tres días" (I want to rent a car for three days)
- "Automático, por favor" (Automatic, please)
- "¿Cuánto cuesta por día?" (How much per day?)
- "¿Qué seguro incluye?" (What insurance does it include?)
- "¿Kilometraje ilimitado?" (Unlimited mileage?)
- "¿A qué hora lo devuelvo?" (What time do I return it?)

### Scenario 2: Filling Up at a Gas Station

Practice getting gas at a service station. Specify fuel type, amount, and handle payment.

Essential phrases:
- "Lleno, por favor" (Fill it up, please)
- "Sin plomo" (Unleaded)
- "Número 3" (Pump number 3)
- "¿Cuánto es?" (How much is it?)
- "¿Dónde está el baño?" (Where is the bathroom?)

### Scenario 3: Getting Directions While Driving

Practice asking for and understanding driving directions. Handle roundabouts, exits, and landmarks.

Essential phrases:
- "¿Cómo llego a Toledo?" (How do I get to Toledo?)
- "¿Por dónde voy?" (Which way do I go?)
- "Estoy perdido" (I'm lost)
- "¿Es esta la carretera a...?" (Is this the road to...?)

### Scenario 4: Handling a Car Problem

Practice reporting a car problem or breakdown. Describe the issue and request assistance.

Essential phrases:
- "El coche no arranca" (The car won't start)
- "Tengo un pinchazo" (I have a flat tire)
- "No funciona el aire acondicionado" (The air conditioning doesn't work)
- "Necesito ayuda" (I need help)
- "¿Puede enviar una grúa?" (Can you send a tow truck?)

## Common Mistakes to Watch

- "Yo quiero rentar" → "Quiero alquilar" (use "alquilar" not "rentar" in Spain)
- Not specifying automatic → Most European cars are manual, always say "automático" if needed
- "Diesel" vs "gasolina" confusion → Critical mistake! Verify fuel type before filling
- Not checking car condition at pickup → Document all scratches/damage or you'll pay later
- Forgetting toll roads cost extra → Spanish highways ("autopistas") often have tolls
- "Lleno de gasolina" → "Lleno, por favor" or "Llenar el depósito" (simpler phrasing)

## Cultural Notes

- **Manual transmission default**: Most rental cars in Spain are manual. Automatic costs significantly more and must be requested specifically.
- **Insurance can be complex**: Basic insurance often has high deductible. "Seguro a todo riesgo" (full coverage) expensive but eliminates worry.
- **Fuel prices per liter**: Gas sold by liter, not gallon. Expect 1.50-2 euros per liter. Full tank ~60-80 euros for small car.
- **Toll roads common**: Major highways ("autopistas" with "AP" designation) usually have tolls. Pay cash or card at booths. Free "autovías" exist but slower.
- **Parking challenges**: City parking very difficult. Use paid parking lots ("aparcamiento"). Street parking often requires ticket from machine.
- **Return car with same fuel**: Must return car with same fuel level as pickup (usually full). Otherwise, hefty refueling fee.
- **International license**: Some agencies require International Driving Permit along with regular license. Check beforehand.
- **Roundabouts everywhere**: Spanish roads love roundabouts. Yield to traffic already in roundabout, exit when you reach your street.
- **Speed cameras common**: Radar cameras on highways. Speed limits enforced strictly. Fines sent to rental company then charged to your card.
- **GPS vs phone**: Rental GPS expensive (10-15 euros/day). Use your phone with offline maps instead (download Spain map before trip).
