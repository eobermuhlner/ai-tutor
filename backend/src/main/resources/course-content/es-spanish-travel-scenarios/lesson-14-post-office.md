## Grammar Focus

### Expressing Destination

**Rule:** Use "a" (to) with countries and cities for destination: "a Estados Unidos" (to United States), "a Londres" (to London).

Examples:
- "Quiero enviar esto a Canadá" (I want to send this to Canada)
- "Una postal a Nueva York" (A postcard to New York)

### Asking About Duration

**Rule:** Use "¿Cuánto tarda?" (How long does it take?) to ask about delivery time.

Examples:
- "¿Cuánto tarda a Estados Unidos?" (How long to United States?)
- "¿Cuántos días?" (How many days?)

## Conversation Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Sending Postcards

Practice sending postcards to friends and family abroad. Buy stamps and understand rates.

Essential phrases:
- "Cinco postales a Estados Unidos, por favor" (Five postcards to United States, please)
- "¿Cuánto cuesta cada sello?" (How much does each stamp cost?)
- "¿Cuánto tarda?" (How long does it take?)
- "¿Puedo echarlas aquí?" (Can I mail them here?)

### Scenario 2: Mailing a Package

Practice sending a package home. Understand weight limits, shipping options, and customs forms.

Essential phrases:
- "Quiero enviar este paquete a Canadá" (I want to send this package to Canada)
- "¿Cuánto pesa?" (How much does it weigh?)
- "¿Cuánto cuesta certificado?" (How much does registered cost?)
- "Necesito el número de seguimiento" (I need the tracking number)

### Scenario 3: Filling Out Customs Forms

Practice completing customs declaration forms for international packages.

Essential phrases:
- "¿Tengo que rellenar un formulario?" (Do I have to fill out a form?)
- "¿Qué pongo aquí?" (What do I put here?)
- "Contiene ropa y recuerdos" (It contains clothes and souvenirs)
- "Valor aproximado" (Approximate value)

### Scenario 4: Buying Stamps

Practice buying stamps at post office or estanco (tobacco shop). Specify quantity and destination.

Essential phrases:
- "Necesito sellos para postales internacionales" (I need stamps for international postcards)
- "Diez sellos, por favor" (Ten stamps, please)
- "Para Europa" (For Europe)
- "¿Los venden aquí?" (Do you sell them here?)

## Common Mistakes to Watch

- "Enviar para Estados Unidos" → "Enviar a Estados Unidos" (use "a" not "para" for destination)
- Forgetting customs forms → International packages always need customs declaration
- Not getting tracking → Always ask for "número de seguimiento" for packages
- Using only post office → Stamps also sold at "estancos" (tobacco shops) - often more convenient
- "¿Cuánto tiempo?" → "¿Cuánto tarda?" (better phrasing for delivery time)
- Not keeping receipt → Essential for tracking and claims if package lost

## Cultural Notes

- **Correos**: Spanish postal service called "Correos." Yellow logo. Reliable but can be slow for international mail.
- **Estancos sell stamps**: Tobacco shops ("estancos") sell stamps. Look for brown and yellow "Tabacos" sign. More convenient than post office.
- **Mailboxes**: Yellow mailboxes for Correos. Two slots usually: one for local mail, one for other destinations. Read labels.
- **Slow international mail**: Postcards to US/Canada can take 2-3 weeks by regular mail. Express much faster but expensive.
- **Customs forms required**: All international packages need customs declaration. Declare honestly - value and contents. Under-declaring can cause problems.
- **Package size limits**: Maximum size/weight limits exist. Very large or heavy packages may need special handling or different service.
- **Post office hours**: Typically 8:30am-2:30pm weekdays, some open Saturday mornings. Closed Sundays. Long lines mid-morning.
- **Tracking**: Certified ("certificado") mail gets tracking. Regular mail doesn't. For valuable items, always use certificado.
- **Receipt important**: Keep receipt ("resguardo") with tracking number. Only proof of mailing if package lost.
- **Private alternatives**: Private companies (DHL, FedEx, MRW) exist but much more expensive. Correos sufficient for most traveler needs.
