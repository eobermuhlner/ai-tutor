## Grammar Focus

### Ordering Drinks

**Rule:** Use "Ponme..." (informal - Put me...) or "Me pone..." (formal - Can you give me...) to order drinks in bars.

Examples:
- "Ponme una cerveza" (Give me a beer - informal)
- "Me pone un vino tinto, por favor" (Can you give me a red wine - formal)
- "Otra, por favor" (Another one, please)

### Suggesting and Accepting

**Rule:** Use "¿Vamos a...?" (Shall we go to...?) to suggest and "Vale" (OK) to accept.

Examples:
- "¿Vamos a tomar algo?" (Shall we go for a drink?)
- "Vale, vamos" (OK, let's go)

## Conversation Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Ordering Drinks at a Bar

Practice ordering drinks for yourself and friends at a bar. Handle rounds and payment.

Essential phrases:
- "Ponme una cerveza, por favor" (Give me a beer, please)
- "Dos gin-tonics" (Two gin and tonics)
- "¿Cuánto es?" (How much is it?)
- "La próxima ronda invito yo" (Next round's on me)
- "Salud" (Cheers)

### Scenario 2: Getting Into a Nightclub

Practice entering a club. Ask about cover charges, dress code, and music style.

Essential phrases:

- "¿Hay lista?" (Is there a guest list?)

- "¿Dónde quedamos?" (Where shall we meet?)
- "A las once" (At eleven)

## Common Mistakes to Watch

- Going out too early → Spanish nightlife starts late! Clubs dead before midnight, peak 2-4am
- "Una copa de cerveza" → "Una cerveza" or "Una caña" (don't say "copa" for beer)
- Not having cash → Many bars cash-only, especially for small drinks
- "La bill" → "La cuenta" (use Spanish word for bill)
- Expecting table service everywhere → In busy bars, order at the bar counter
- Drinking tap water after drinks → Ask for "agua" - servers expect this, it's normal

## Cultural Notes

- **Late, late night**: Spanish nightlife starts late. Dinner at 10pm, bars at midnight, clubs at 1-2am. Clubs peak 3am, close 6-7am.
- **Free tapas with drinks**: In some regions (Granada, León), each drink comes with free tapa. In others (Madrid, Barcelona), tapas cost extra.
- **Drinking on the street**: "Botellón" (drinking in public) technically illegal in most cities but still common. Bars sell bottles to-go.
- **Age requirement**: Legal drinking age 18. ID rarely checked unless you look very young.
- **Bar hopping**: Spanish go to multiple venues in one night. Start at bars, move to clubs later. Normal to visit 3-5 places.
- **Paying for drinks**: Often pay per drink, not tab. Some places give you ticket you pay at end. Ask "¿Pago ahora?" (Do I pay now?)
- **Tipping**: Round up or leave small change. Not expected but appreciated. 5-10% for good service.
- **Safety**: Spain generally very safe, but watch your belongings in crowded bars/clubs. Pickpockets target drunk tourists.
- **Dress code**: Clubs may have dress code (no sneakers/shorts for men). Casual-smart usually fine. Ask hotel for advice.
- **Metro hours**: Metro closes around 1:30am weekdays, runs all night Fridays/Saturdays in Madrid. Check your city. Night buses ("búhos") exist.
