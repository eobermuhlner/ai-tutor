## This Week's Goals

- Book tours and activities online or in person
- Ask about schedules, prices, and what's included
- Make reservations and confirmations
- Understand meeting points and requirements
- Handle cancellations or changes

## Grammar Focus

### Making Reservations

**Rule:** Use "Quiero reservar..." (I want to book...) or "Me gustaría reservar..." (I would like to book...).

Examples:
- "Quiero reservar un tour" (I want to book a tour)
- "Me gustaría reservar para cuatro personas" (I'd like to book for four people)

### Asking What's Included

**Rule:** Use "¿Qué incluye?" (What does it include?) and "¿Está incluido...?" (Is... included?).

Examples:
- "¿Qué incluye el tour?" (What does the tour include?)
- "¿Está incluida la comida?" (Is food included?)

## Conversation Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Booking a Food Tour

Practice booking a food tour. Ask about schedule, price, what's included, and meeting point.

Essential phrases:
- "Quiero reservar el tour gastronómico" (I want to book the food tour)
- "Para mañana, dos personas" (For tomorrow, two people)
- "¿A qué hora empieza?" (What time does it start?)
- "¿Qué incluye?" (What does it include?)
- "¿Dónde quedamos?" (Where do we meet?)

### Scenario 2: Booking a Flamenco Show

Practice reserving seats for a flamenco show. Ask about show times, seating options, and drinks.

Essential phrases:
- "¿Hay entradas para esta noche?" (Are there tickets for tonight?)
- "Dos personas" (Two people)
- "¿Incluye bebida?" (Does it include a drink?)
- "¿A qué hora empieza el espectáculo?" (What time does the show start?)
- "¿Dónde está el tablao?" (Where is the venue?)

### Scenario 3: Cooking Class Reservation

Practice booking a cooking class. Ask what you'll cook, what's included, and what to bring.

Essential phrases:
- "Me gustaría reservar la clase de cocina" (I'd like to book the cooking class)
- "¿Qué vamos a cocinar?" (What are we going to cook?)
- "¿Está incluida la comida después?" (Is the meal afterwards included?)
- "¿Qué necesito traer?" (What do I need to bring?)
- "¿Para cuántas personas máximo?" (For how many people maximum?)

### Scenario 4: Changing or Canceling a Reservation

Practice modifying or canceling a booked activity. Understand cancellation policies and refunds.

Essential phrases:
- "Necesito cambiar mi reserva" (I need to change my reservation)
- "¿Puedo cambiar la fecha?" (Can I change the date?)
- "Quiero cancelar" (I want to cancel)
- "¿Hay reembolso?" (Is there a refund?)
- "El número de reserva es..." (The reservation number is...)

## Practice Patterns

- Successfully book tour or activity with all necessary details
- Understand what's included and what you need to bring
- Confirm meeting point and arrival time
- Modify or cancel reservation according to policies
- Navigate confirmation emails and vouchers

## Common Mistakes to Watch

- "Reservar para dos" → "Reservar para dos personas" (specify "personas")
- Not asking about cancellation → Always ask "¿Política de cancelación?" before booking
- Forgetting to ask meeting point → "¿Dónde quedamos?" essential! Don't assume you know
- "¿Qué hora empieza?" → "¿A qué hora empieza?" (need "a")
- Not clarifying pickup → Ask "¿Recogida en hotel?" or "¿Vamos por nuestra cuenta?"
- Assuming everything included → Always ask "¿Qué incluye?" and "¿Qué NO incluye?"

## Cultural Notes

- **Book ahead**: Popular activities (flamenco shows, wine tours, cooking classes) book up, especially summer. Reserve 1-3 days ahead minimum.
- **Small group tours better**: Private or small group (8-12 people) tours more expensive but much better experience than big bus tours (40+ people).
- **Free walking tours**: Many cities have "free" walking tours (tip-based). You pay what you think it's worth at end. Usually excellent value.
- **Hotel can book**: Hotels often happy to book tours/activities for you. They may get commission but it's convenient. Or book direct to support local businesses.
- **Flamenco shows range**: Tourist-trap tablaos charge 40-70 euros. Authentic local venues 15-25 euros. Ask hotel or locals for recommendations.
- **Wine tours**: Rioja, Ribera del Duero, Priorat regions famous for wine. Tours include transport, winery visits, tastings, lunch. Full-day usually 60-100 euros.
- **Cooking classes**: Popular in Madrid, Barcelona, San Sebastián. Usually 3-4 hours, you cook traditional dishes, then eat what you made. 50-90 euros typical.
- **Food tours**: Tapas tours in every major city. Walk to 4-5 bars, taste local specialties, learn culture. Great introduction. 40-80 euros, 3-4 hours.
- **Cancellation policies**: Read carefully! Many "free cancellation up to 24 hours." Some charge 50% if you cancel within 48 hours. Know before booking.
- **Show voucher on phone**: Most bookings don't require printing. Email confirmation or voucher on phone accepted. But have backup (screenshot) in case no wifi.
