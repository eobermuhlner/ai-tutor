## Lesson Goals

- Buy bus tickets for intercity or local buses
- Understand bus schedules and departure bays
- Ask about routes and connections
- Find your bus and board correctly
- Communicate with drivers and station staff

## Grammar Focus

### Asking About Routes

**Rule:** Use "¿Qué autobús va a...?" (Which bus goes to...?) to ask about routes.

Examples:
- "¿Qué autobús va al centro?" (Which bus goes to the center?)
- "¿Este autobús va a Granada?" (Does this bus go to Granada?)
- "¿Pasa por el aeropuerto?" (Does it pass by the airport?)

### Expressing Frequency

**Rule:** Use "cada" (every) for frequency: "Cada hora" (Every hour), "Cada 30 minutos" (Every 30 minutes).

Examples:
- "¿Cada cuánto sale?" (How often does it leave?)
- "Sale cada media hora" (It leaves every half hour)

## Conversation Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Buying a Bus Ticket

Practice buying an intercity bus ticket at the station. Specify your destination, preferred departure time, and whether you need one-way or round-trip.

Essential phrases:
- "Quiero un billete a Sevilla" (I want a ticket to Seville)
- "Para hoy" (For today)
- "¿Cuánto cuesta?" (How much does it cost?)
- "¿A qué hora sale el próximo?" (What time does the next one leave?)
- "Ida y vuelta" (Round-trip)

### Scenario 2: Finding the Right Bus

Practice confirming which bus goes to your destination and finding the correct platform or bay. Ask about route and stops.

Essential phrases:
- "¿Qué autobús va al aeropuerto?" (Which bus goes to the airport?)
- "¿De qué andén sale?" (Which bay does it leave from?)
- "¿Este autobús va a Granada?" (Does this bus go to Granada?)
- "¿Pasa por el centro?" (Does it pass by the center?)

### Scenario 3: On the Bus

Practice communicating with the driver about your stop. Ask to be notified when you reach your destination, especially if you're unfamiliar with the route.

Essential phrases:
- "¿Me puede avisar cuando lleguemos a...?" (Can you let me know when we arrive at...?)
- "Quiero bajar en la próxima" (I want to get off at the next stop)
- "¿Falta mucho para...?" (Is it much longer until...?)
- "¿Dónde me bajo para el museo?" (Where do I get off for the museum?)

### Scenario 4: Local City Bus

Practice taking a local city bus. Ask about frequency, pay the driver directly, and understand how to signal your stop.

Essential phrases:
- "¿Este autobús va al centro?" (Does this bus go to downtown?)
- "¿Cuánto es?" (How much is it?)
- "¿Cada cuánto pasa?" (How often does it run?)
- "La próxima parada, por favor" (Next stop, please)

## Practice Patterns

- Purchase an intercity bus ticket with all necessary details
- Find and board the correct bus using only Spanish
- Successfully navigate a local city bus route to a destination
- Ask driver to notify you at your stop and get off correctly
- Handle a schedule change or find alternative bus times

## Common Mistakes to Watch

- "Autobús para Madrid" → "Autobús a Madrid" (use "a" for destinations)
- Not having exact change for city buses → Many drivers don't accept large bills
- Confusing "subir" (get on) with "bajar" (get off) → Critical for understanding directions!
- "¿Cuándo llega?" → "¿Cuánto tarda?" (ask "how long" not "when arrives" for duration)
- Not validating ticket on city buses → Some cities require validation in machine on board
- Saying "parar" instead of "bajar" → Use "quiero bajar" not "quiero parar"

## Cultural Notes

- **Two types of buses**: "Autobuses" (intercity/long-distance) from stations, and city buses from street stops. Different systems, different tickets.
- **Intercity buses**: Usually cheaper than trains for same routes. Companies like ALSA are common. Book at station or online.
- **City buses**: Pay driver when boarding. Have exact change or small bills. Large bills often not accepted.
- **Bus stations vs stops**: "Estación de autobuses" is the main terminal for intercity buses. "Parada" is a city bus stop on the street.
- **No eating on buses**: Generally not allowed to eat or drink on intercity buses. Water is usually okay.
- **Luggage storage**: Intercity buses have luggage compartments underneath. Keep valuables with you.
- **Request stops**: On city buses, press the button or say "la próxima parada, por favor" to request a stop.
- **Punctuality varies**: Intercity buses usually run on schedule. City buses can be less predictable during peak hours.
- **Multiple companies**: Different bus companies may serve same routes with different schedules and prices. Compare options.
- **"Billete" vs "bonobús"**: Single tickets are "billetes". Multi-ride cards for city buses are "bonobús" or "tarjeta" - much cheaper if staying longer.
