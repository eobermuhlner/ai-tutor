## Grammar Focus

**Comedic Expression Constructions:** Using onomatopoeia and mimetic words for comedic effect

**Rule:** Korean humor often uses onomatopoeia (words that imitate sounds) and mimetic words (words that describe states or actions) to enhance comedic moments

Examples:
- **킁킁 (kkwikkwi)** - sound of sniffing (often used for comedic effect)
- **낄낄 (kkilkil)** - sound of giggling/chuckling
- **풉 (puk)** - sound of suppressed laughter

This lesson continues our vocabulary-focused approach by emphasizing humor terms essential for understanding comedic moments in manhwa.

- **Self-deprecating** - 자학 개그 (jakhak gaegeu) - self-deprecating jokes
- **Dry humor** - 냉철한 유머 (naengcheolhan yumeo) - dry/cold humor
- **Nonsensical** - 무의미한 유머 (mui-mihan yumeo) - meaningless humor
- **Satire** - 풍자 (pungja) - satire/parody
- **Parody** - 패러디 (paereodi) - parody (from English "parody")
- **Irony** - 아이러니 (aireoni) - irony (from English "irony")

### Comedic Dialogue
- **What?** - 뭐? (mwo?) - what? (in surprise)
- **Really?** - 정말? (jeongmal?) - really? (in surprise)
- **Are you serious?** - 진짜? (jinjja?) - really? (in disbelief)
- **I don't know** - 몰라 (molla) - don't know (casual)
- **I have no idea** - 전혀 몰라 (cheonjeo molla) - don't know at all
- **I don't get it** - 이해가 안 돼 (ihaega an dwae) - don't understand
- **That's weird** - 이상해 (isanghae) - it's strange/weird
- **That's ridiculous** - 말도 안 돼 (maldo an dwae) - doesn't make sense/ridiculous
- **You're kidding** - 농담이에요? (nongdameiyo?) - Are you kidding?
- **No way** - 안 되지 (an doejiji) - No way (in disbelief)

- **lose words** - 말이 나오지 않다 (mari nachiji anta) - words don't come out

## Cultural Notes

1. **Physical Comedy:** Korean manhwa often features exaggerated physical reactions
2. **"Aegyo" Humor:** Cutesy behavior that can be funny in certain contexts
3. **"Nunchi" Comedy:** Humor based on reading social situations
4. **Wordplay Culture:** Korean has many puns and wordplay that are culturally specific
5. **Embarrassment Culture:** Korean humor often involves socially awkward situations
6. **Self-Deprecation:** The acceptance of self-deprecating humor in Korean culture
7. **"Tteokbokki" Humor:** Common Korean "dad jokes" that are intentionally corny
