## Lesson Goals

- Understand different manhwa genres and types
- Learn essential vocabulary for describing characters and panels
- Get familiar with common manhwa expressions and formatting

## Grammar Focus

**Simple Noun Identification:** Use of basic nouns with context clues

**Rule:** Korean nouns can be identified by their meaning in context, often followed by particles that indicate their role in the sentence.

Examples:
- **만화 (manhwa)** - comic/webtoon
- **웹툰 (webtoon)** - web comic (from English "webtoon")
- **일러스트 (illustrateu)** - illustration

This course will focus on vocabulary acquisition and context recognition rather than extensive grammar study, as these skills are most important for reading manhwa.

## Vocabulary

### Manhwa Genres
- **로맨스 (romanseu)** - romance
- **액션 (aekseon)** - action
- **판타지 (pantaji)** - fantasy
- **드라마 (deurama)** - drama
- **학원 (hakwon)** - school/campus
- **스릴러 (seurilleo)** - thriller
- **코미디 (komidi)** - comedy
- **무협 (muhap)** - martial arts
- **시대 (sidae)** - historical
- **BL (bi el)** - Boys Love (same-sex romance between males)
- **GL (ji el)** - Girls Love (same-sex romance between females)
- ** slice of life ** - 일상 (ilsang) - daily life

### Character Expressions
- **표정 (pyojeong)** - expression
- **미소 (miso)** - smile
- **웃음 (useum)** - laugh/laughter
- **눈물 (nunmul)** - tears
- **분노 (bunno)** - anger/rage
- **슬픔 (seulpeum)** - sadness
- **놀람 (nolam)** - surprise
- **실신 (shilsin)** - fainting
- **심장이 쿵쾅쿵쾅 (simjangi kungkwak kungkwak)** - heart beating loudly
- ** blush ** - 홍조 (hongjo) - blush (redness on cheeks)

### Panel and Format Terms
- **패널 (paeneol)** - panel
- **만화책 (manhwachat)** - comic book
- **이미지 (imiji)** - image
- **말풍선 (malpungseon)** - speech bubble
- **생각 말풍선 (saenggak malpungseon)** - thought bubble
- **배경 (baegyeong)** - background
- **자막 (jamat)** - caption/text box
- **효과 (hyogwa)** - effect
- **선 (seon)** - line
- **색 (saek)** - color
- **흑백 (heukbaek)** - black and white
- **컬러 (keollo)** - color (from English "color")

### Character Descriptions
- **주인공 (ju-ingong)** - main character/protagonist
- **악당 (akdang)** - villain/bad guy
- **서포터 (seoputeo)** - supporter/ally
- **히로인 (hiroin)** - heroine
- **남자 주인공 (namja ju-ingong)** - male protagonist
- **여자 주인공 (yeoja ju-ingong)** - female protagonist
- **조연 (joyeon)** - supporting character
- **단역 (danyeok)** - minor character
- **외형 (oehyeong)** - appearance/looks
- **성격 (seonggyeok)** - personality

## Conversation Scenarios

As this course focuses on reading manhwa rather than daily conversation, the AI tutor will help you understand and identify vocabulary used in different manhwa scenes and contexts. You will practice recognizing terms for different genres, character expressions, and panel compositions.

Scenarios to practice:
- Identifying genre and mood from panel descriptions
- Understanding character expressions and emotions
- Recognizing common manhwa formatting elements

AI tutor will present descriptions of manhwa scenes and you will identify relevant vocabulary and concepts.

## Practice Patterns

1. **Genre Recognition:** Look at manhwa covers and identify the genre based on visual cues and vocabulary
2. **Expression Identification:** Study character faces and match expressions with Korean terms
3. **Format Terms Practice:** Learn to identify different panel types and formatting elements
4. **Character Identification:** Distinguish between protagonist, antagonist, and supporting characters
5. **Vocabulary Matching:** Match Korean terms with their English equivalents

## Common Mistakes to Watch

- **Reading Direction Confusion:** Manhwa read top to bottom, right to left (unlike Western comics)
- **Slang vs Formal:** Understanding when informal expressions are used in dialogue
- **Onomatopoeia:** Recognizing sound effects which often use Chinese characters (hanja) or creative spelling
- **Character vs Narration:** Distinguishing between character dialogue and narrator text
- **Cultural Context:** Understanding Korean-specific cultural references that may not be familiar to non-Korean readers

## Cultural Notes

1. **Manhwa vs Manga:** Korean manhwa developed separately from Japanese manga, with different artistic and storytelling traditions
2. **Webtoon Format:** Korean webtoons are optimized for smartphone reading, with vertical scrolling format
3. **Genre Preferences:** BL (Boys Love) and GL (Girls Love) have significant audiences in Korea and are mainstream genres
4. **Artistic Styles:** Korean manhwa often features more realistic character designs compared to Japanese manga
5. **Platform Culture:** Many popular manhwa start on web platforms like Naver Webtoon or Lezhin Comics
6. **Age Ratings:** Korean manhwa have clear age ratings (all ages, 15+, 18+) that indicate content appropriateness
7. **Episode Structure:** Manhwa chapters are typically released weekly with consistent numbering
