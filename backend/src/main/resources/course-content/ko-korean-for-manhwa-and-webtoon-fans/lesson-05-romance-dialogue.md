## Lesson Goals

- Learn common romantic expressions used in manhwa
- Understand terms related to love confessions and relationships
- Master dialogue patterns used in romantic situations

## Grammar Focus

**Romantic Expression Constructions:** Using "na" particles and honorifics in romantic contexts

**Rule:** Korean romantic expressions often use soft, gentle language with specific particles and honorifics to convey affection and respect

Examples:
- **사랑해 (saranghae)** - I love you (casual)
- **사랑합니다 (saranghamnida)** - I love you (formal/honorific)
- **좋아해 (joaha)** - I like you (casual)
- **좋아합니다 (joahamnida)** - I like you (formal)

This lesson continues our focus on vocabulary while introducing important romantic expressions used frequently in manhwa.

## Vocabulary

### Love Expressions
- **사랑 (sarang)** - love
- **사랑해 (saranghae)** - I love you (informal)
- **사랑합니다 (saranghamnida)** - I love you (formal)
- **사랑에 빠지다 (sarange ppajida)** - to fall in love
- **사랑꾼 (sarangkkun)** - lover/romantic partner
- **연애 (yeonae)** - romance/love affair
- **사귀다 (saguida)** - to date/go out with
- ** 애정 (aejeong)** - affection/love
- **정 (jeong)** - affection/love (Korean concept)

### Romantic Feelings
- ** Crush ** - 애정 (aejeong) - affection, crush
- **썸 (sseom)** - having a thing for someone/crush
- **짝사랑 (jjaksarang)** - one-sided love/unrequited love
- **썸 타다 (sseom tada)** - to have a crush on someone
- ** 맘 (mam)** - heart/feeling (colloquial)
- ** 마음 (ma-eum)** - heart/mind/feeling (formal)
- ** 마음을 주다 (ma-eumeul juda)** - to give one's heart
- ** 맘을 얻다 (mameul eotda)** - to win someone's heart
- ** 맘을 뺏다 (mameul ppetda)** - to steal someone's heart
- ** 심장이 뛴다 (simjangi ttwinda)** - heart is beating (for someone)

### Confession Terms
- **고백 (gobaek)** - confession/declaration of love
- **사랑 고백 (sarang gobaek)** - love confession
- **고백하다 (gobaekhada)** - to confess
- **고백하다 (gobaekhada)** - to express love
- **고백하는 장소 (gobaekhaneun jangso)** - confession spot
- **고백의 장소 (gobaegui jangso)** - place of confession
- **기회 (gihoe)** - chance/opportunity (for confession)
- **용기 (yonggi)** - courage (to confess)
- **용기를 내다 (yonggireul naeda)** - to gather courage
- ** 진심 (jinsim)** - sincere/honest feelings

### Romantic Actions
- **키스 (kiseu)** - kiss (from English "kiss")
- **뽀뽀 (ppoppo)** - kiss (cute/slang)
- **꽃다 (kkotda)** - to bloom (figuratively, for feelings)
- **속삭이다 (soksagida)** - to whisper
- ** 귓속말 (gwitsokmal)** - whisper in ear
- ** 손을 잡다 (soneul jappa)** - to hold hands
- ** 허벅지를 만지다 (heobakjireul manjida)** - to touch thigh (intimate)
- ** 껴안다 (kkyeondanda)** - to embrace/hug
- ** 꼭 안다 (kkok andanda)** - to hug tightly
- ** 감싸다 (gampsada)** - to wrap/cuddle

### Romantic Scenes and Situations
- **데이트 (deiteu)** - date (from English "date")
- **로맨스 (romanseu)** - romance
- **로맨틱 (romanteuk)** - romantic (from English)
- **밀당 (mildang)** - romantic push and pull
- **썸 중 (sseom jung)** - in the middle of a crush/romance
- **썸남 (sseomnam)** - crush guy
- **썸녀 (sseomnyeo)** - crush girl
- **러브라인 (reobeulain)** - love line (romantic subplot)
- **러브 트라이앵글 (reobeu teuraiyanggeul)** - love triangle
- **러브 스토리 (reobeu seutori)** - love story

### Romantic Feelings and Emotions
- ** 설렘 (seollem)** - flutter/anticipation (unique Korean emotion)
- ** 떨림 (tteollim)** - trembling/excitement
- ** 가슴 설레다 (gaseum seolleda)** - heart flutters
- ** 심장이 쿵쾅 (simjangi kungkwak)** - heart pounds
- ** 심장이 뛰다 (simjangi twida)** - heart beats fast
- ** 가슴이 두근두근 (gaseumi dugeundugeun)** - heart beats fast (onomatopoeia)
- ** 가슴이 설렌다 (gaseumi seollenda)** - heart is fluttering
- ** 얼굴이 붉어지다 (eolguri bulgeojida)** - face turns red/blushes
- ** 뺨이 뜨겁다 (ppyami tteugeopda)** - cheeks are hot
- ** 어지럽다 (eojireopda)** - dizzy/overwhelmed (with emotion)

### Romantic Dialogue Phrases
- **나도 (nado)** - me too (I feel the same way)
- ** 나도 그래 (nado geurae)** - I feel that way too
- ** 나도 좋아해 (nado joaha)** - I like you too
- ** 나도 사랑해 (nado saranghae)** - I love you too
- ** 나도 기다렸어 (nado gidaryeosseo)** - I've been waiting for this too
- ** 나도 기다려 (nado gidaryeo)** - I'm waiting too (for a response)
- ** 기다리다 (gidarida)** - to wait (for you)
- ** 기다릴게 (gidarilge)** - I'll wait (for you)
- ** 함께 (hamkke)** - together
- ** 영원히 (yeongwonhi)** - forever

### Intimate Expressions
- ** 너를 사랑해 (neoreul saranghae)** - I love you (you)
- ** 나의 사람 (naui saram)** - my person
- ** 내 사랑 (nae sarang)** - my love
- ** 내 사람 (nae saram)** - my person
- ** 달콤 (dal-kkom)** - sweet
- ** 달달 (dal-dal)** - sweetly (romance scene slang)
- ** 달콤한 (dal-kkomhan)** - sweet
- ** 달콤하게 (dal-kkomhage)** - sweetly
- ** 달콤하게 만들다 (dal-kkomhage mandeulda)** - to make sweet

### Romantic Rejection and Heartbreak
- ** 거절 (geojeol)** - rejection
- ** 거절하다 (geojeolhada)** - to reject
- ** 거절당하다 (geojeoldanghada)** - to be rejected
- ** 상처 (sangcheo)** - wound/heartbreak
- ** 실연 (siryeon)** - heartbreak/rejection
- ** 짝사랑 끝 (jjaksarang kkeut)** - end of unrequited love
- ** 싫어 (sireo)** - hate/don't like
- ** 싫어하지 않다 (sireohaji anta)** - don't dislike (positive way of saying you like)
- ** 마음이 없다 (ma-eumi eoptda)** - don't have feelings for you
- ** 마음을 주지 못하다 (ma-eumeul jusi mothada)** - can't give my heart

- **Cultural Context:** Missing cultural nuances in romantic expressions

2. **Indirect Communication:** Korean romantic expressions often use indirect language
3. **Age Hierarchy:** Romantic expressions change based on age relationships
4. **Physical Boundaries:** What is considered acceptable physical intimacy in Korean culture
5. **Confession Culture:** The importance of formal confessions ("saseng") in Korean romance
6. **Romantic Timing:** When it's appropriate to express romantic feelings in Korean culture
