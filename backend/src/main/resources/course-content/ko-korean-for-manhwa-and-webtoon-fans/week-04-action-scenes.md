- Learn vocabulary for action verbs and movements in manhwa

- Master expressions for speed, impact, and intensity in action scenes

## Grammar Focus

**Action Verb Conjugations:** Understanding present and past tense for action verbs

**Rule:** Korean action verbs often change form depending on the intensity or completion of the action, especially in action scenes

Examples:
- **날다 (naldah)** - to fly (present)
- **날아오르다 (naraoreuda)** - to soar upward (intensified form)
- **쫓다 (jotda)** - to chase
- **쫓아가다 (jochagada)** - to chase after/run after

This lesson continues our vocabulary-focused approach by emphasizing action-related terms essential for understanding dynamic scenes in manhwa.

## Vocabulary

### Basic Movement Verbs
- **걷다 (geotda)** - to walk
- **달리다 (dallida)** - to run
- **뛰다 (twida)** - to jump
- **앉다 (andda)** - to sit
- **서다 (seoda)** - to stand
- **눕다 (nupda)** - to lie down
- **굽다 (gupda)** - to kneel/bend
- **휘청거리다 (hwicheonggeorida)** - to stagger/wobble
- **비틀거리다 (biteulgeorida)** - to stumble
- **기어가다 (gieogada)** - to crawl

### Advanced Movement
- **날다 (naldah)** - to fly
- **휩쓸다 (hwipsseulda)** - to sweep/swipe
- **휩돌다 (hwipdolda)** - to whirl/swirl
- **휩싸다 (hwipssta)** - to wrap around
- **뻗다 (ppeolda)** - to stretch out
- ** coiling ** - 말다 (malda) - to coil/wrap
- **뻗치다 (ppyeolchida)** - to stretch out/flare out
- **꺾다 (kkeokda)** - to bend/break
- **부딪치다 (buditchida)** - to collide/hit against
- **튕기다 (ttaenggida)** - to bounce/kick off

### Combat and Fighting
- **싸우다 (ssauda)** - to fight
- **싸움 (ssaum)** - fight
- ** pecking ** - 쪼다 (jjoda) - to peck/bite
- **치다 (chida)** - to hit/strike
- **치근거리다 (chigeungeorida)** - to struggle/fight
- **격투 (gyeoktu)** - combat/fighting
- **격파 (gyeokpa)** - to break through/defeat
- **공격 (gonggyeok)** - attack
- **방어 (bang-eo)** - defense
- **반격 (bangyeok)** - counterattack

### Combat Actions
- **펀치 (peonchi)** - punch (from English "punch")
- **킥 (kik)** - kick (from English "kick")
- **찌르다 (jjireuda)** - to stab/pierce
- **뻗치다 (ppyeolchida)** - to thrust/kick out
- **휩쓸다 (hwipsseulda)** - to sweep/swipe
- **뻗다 (ppeolda)** - to extend/reach out (in fighting)
- **받아치다 (badachida)** - to parry and counter
- **막다 (makda)** - to block
- **회피하다 (hoepihada)** - to dodge/evade
- **구르다 (gureuda)** - to roll/dodge

### Weapon-Based Combat
- **칼 (kal)** - knife/sword
- **무기 (mugi)** - weapon
- **검 (geom)** - sword
- **창 (chang)** - spear
- **도끼 (dokki)** - axe
- **활 (hwal)** - bow
- **화살 (hwasal)** - arrow
- **총 (chong)** - gun
- **단도 (dando)** - dagger
- **부적 (bujeok)** - charm/amulet

### Impact and Force
- **쾅 (kwang)** - sound of impact
- **퍽 (peok)** - sound of hitting
- **퍽퍽 (peokpeok)** - sound of multiple hits
- **와장창 (wangjangchang)** - sound of breaking/crashing
- **솨 (swa)** - sound of slicing
- **좍 (jjuk)** - sound of cutting
- **쾅쾅 (kwangkwang)** - repeated heavy impact
- **퍽퍽 (peokpeok)** - repeated hitting sounds
- **와장 (wangjang)** - sound of shattering

### Speed and Intensity
- **빠르게 (ppareuge)** - quickly/rapidly
- **천천히 (cheoncheonhi)** - slowly/cautiously
- **신속히 (shinsokhi)** - promptly/swiftly
- **즉시 (jeuksi)** - immediately
- **순식간에 (sunsigane)** - in an instant
- **우수 (usu)** - excellence/skill
- **신기 (shinki)** - skillful/remarkable
- **능숙 (neungsuk)** - skillful/proficient
- **화려 (hwaroe)** - splendid/brilliant
- **화려히 (hwaroehi)** - brilliantly/splendidly

### Special Movement Effects
- **번쩍 (beonjjeok)** - flash/suddenly appear
- **휙 (hwik)** - swish/swift motion
- **슥 (seuk)** - sudden quick movement
- **휙휙 (hwikhwik)** - rapid back and forth motion
- **솨솨 (swaswa)** - slicing sounds
- **솨르륵 (swareureok)** - smooth sliding sound
- **솨아 (swaa)** - dramatic impact sound
- **휘이 (hwii)** - swift movement sound
- **솨르르 (swareureu)** - flowing sound
- **솨적 (swajeok)** - clean slicing sound

2. **Combat Identification:** Recognize fighting techniques and combat styles
3. **Sound Effect Matching:** Match sound effects to appropriate actions
4. **Intensity Assessment:** Identify how intensity is conveyed in action scenes
5. **Speed Description:** Understand how speed is described in manhwa

## Common Mistakes to Watch

- **Sound Effect Confusion:** Not understanding the difference between similar onomatopoeia
- **Action Context:** Misunderstanding the context of actions without visual cues
- **Cultural References:** Missing cultural references in fighting styles or techniques
- **Intensity Levels:** Not recognizing when actions are exaggerated for effect
- **Technical Terms:** Confusing similar terms for different types of movements

## Cultural Notes

1. **Martial Arts (무술):** Korean martial arts often feature in manhwa, with specific terminology for techniques
2. **Weapon Culture:** Traditional Korean weapons and their uses in historical manhwa
3. **Speed Perception:** Korean culture's appreciation for quick, decisive action in combat
4. **Chivalry:** Honor codes that may appear in certain combat scenes
5. **Training Concepts:** The importance of training and skill development in action manhwa
6. **Sound Effects:** How sound effects in Korean manhwa differ from those in other cultures
7. **Impact Expression:** The cultural significance of showing impact and power in visual storytelling
