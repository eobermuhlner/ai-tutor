## Lesson Goals

- Learn vocabulary for different character types in manhwa
- Understand common locations and settings
- Master terms related to manhwa storytelling and narrative elements

## Grammar Focus

**Basic Adjective Descriptions:** Using adjectives to describe characteristics

**Rule:** Korean adjectives directly modify nouns or come before them in sentences

Examples:
- **사랑스러운 (sarangseureoun)** - lovable/cute
- **무서운 (museoun)** - scary/frightening
- **재미있는 (jaemi inneun)** - interesting/fun

This lesson continues our focus on vocabulary acquisition for manhwa reading comprehension.

## Vocabulary

### Character Types and Roles
- **주인공 (ju-ingong)** - protagonist/main character
- **2차 주인공 (2cha ju-ingong)** - secondary protagonist
- ** antagonist ** - 반대자 (banda-ja) - opposing person
- **조연 (joyeon)** - supporting character
- **단역 (danyeok)** - minor character
- **악역 (akyeok)** - villain/antagonist
- **악당 (akdang)** - bad guy/villain
- **히어로 (hieo)** - hero
- **히로인 (hiroin)** - heroine
- **사랑꾼 (sarangkkun)** - lover/romantic interest
- **라이벌 (raibul)** - rival
- **서포터 (seopoteo)** - supporter/ally
- **가족 (gajok)** - family
- **친구 (chingu)** - friend
- **선생님 (seonsaengnim)** - teacher
- **후배 (hubae)** - junior (in school/workplace hierarchy)
- **선배 (seonbae)** - senior (in school/workplace hierarchy)
- **후궁 (hugung)** - royal concubine (in historical manhwa)
- **왕 (wang)** - king
- **왕비 (wangbi)** - queen

## Practice Patterns

1. **Character Recognition:** Identify character roles (protagonist, villain, friend) from descriptions
2. **Setting Identification:** Recognize common manhwa locations from scene descriptions
3. **Story Elements:** Identify plot elements like flashbacks, plot twists, and time periods
4. **Relationship Mapping:** Understand character relationships and romantic dynamics
5. **Physical Description Practice:** Describe characters using appropriate adjectives

## Common Mistakes to Watch

- **Hierarchy Confusion:** Mixing up 'sunbae' (senior) and 'hubae' (junior) relationships which are important in Korean culture
- **Cultural Context:** Not understanding the significance of family relationships in Korean stories
- **Formal vs Informal:** Confusing formal and informal expressions used between different character types
- **Time Context:** Misunderstanding temporal terms like "flashback" or "foreshadowing"
- **Genre Expectations:** Not recognizing when certain relationship dynamics are typical of specific genres

## Cultural Notes

1. **Sunbae-Hubae:** The senior-junior relationship is very important in Korean culture and frequently appears in school and workplace manhwa
2. **Family Honor:** Family relationships and honor often play crucial roles in Korean narratives
3. **Social Hierarchy:** Understanding class differences and social positions is important for reading many manhwa
4. **School Culture:** Korean schools have specific traditions and social dynamics that appear frequently in school-based manhwa
5. **Age Terms:** Korean has different terms for relatives based on age relationships (older brother vs younger brother)
6. **Romance Terms:** Korean has specific terms for romantic concepts that may not have direct English equivalents
7. **Respect Language:** Characters use different language levels based on social position, which affects word choice and endings
