## Grammar Focus

**Advanced Dialogue Constructions:** Using formal/informal speech levels and character-specific expressions

**Rule:** Korean dialogue varies significantly based on social hierarchy, age, gender, and character archetypes. Different characters use distinct speech patterns that reflect their personality and status.

Examples:
- **Formal:** ~합니다 (hamnida) ending - polite, respectful
- **Informal:** ~해 (hae) ending - casual, familiar
- **Gender-specific:** Different expressions used by male/female characters
- **Age hierarchy:** Language changes based on speaker/addressee age relationship

This lesson combines our previous vocabulary knowledge with advanced dialogue patterns used in Korean manhwa.

## Vocabulary

### Formal vs Informal Language
- **Formal ending:** ~합니다 (hamnida) - most polite ending
- **Polite ending:** ~어요/아요 (eoyo/ayo) - polite but less formal
- **Casual ending:** ~해 (hae) - informal with friends/equal status
- **Honorific language:** 높임말 (nophim-mal) - respectful language
- **Humble language:** 낮춤말 (najim-mal) - humble language about oneself
- **Intimate language:** 반말 (banmal) - informal language (close relationships)
- **Respectful verb forms:** ~시 (si) - honorific marker
- **Formal verb endings:** ~(으)ㄴ다 - written/formal speech
- **Casual speech:** ~(으)ㄴ다 → ~는다 - spoken informal
- **Contraction patterns:** Formal to informal contractions

### Character-Specific Speech Patterns
- **Tsundere** - 따뜻함을 잘 표현 못하는 성격 (dattaum-eul joh seonghyeon mothaneun seonggyeok) - hard to show warmth personality
- **Kuudere** - 냉혈한 성격 (naenghyeolhan seonggyeok) - cold-blooded personality
- **Dandere** - 조용하고 내성적인 성격 (joyonghago naeseongjeogin seonggyeok) - quiet and introverted personality
- **Kamidere** - 자신감 있는 성격 (jachimgeum inneun seonggyeok) - confident personality
- **Yandere** - 사랑에 지나치게 몰입하는 성격 (sarange ganachige moolipaneun seonggyeok) - overly devoted personality
- **Stoic male:** 말수가 적은 성격 (malsuga jeog-eun seonggyeok) - few-word personality
- **Tsundere female:** 따뜻함을 감추는 성격 (dattaum-eul gamchuneun seonggyeok) - warmth-hiding personality
- **Cheerful character:** 밝고 긍정적인 성격 (balgo geungjeongjeogin seonggyeok) - bright and positive personality
- **Serious character:** 진지한 성격 (jinjihan seonggyeok) - serious personality
- **Playful character:** 장난꾸러기 성격 (jangnan-kkeureogi seonggyeok) - mischievous personality

### Formal Address Terms
- **You (polite):** ~님 (nim) - respectful suffix
- **You (very formal):** 귀하 (gwiha) - honorific "you"
- **Self-reference (humble):** 저 (jeo) - humble "I"
- **Self-reference (formal):** 저는 (jeoneun) - formal "I am"
- **Self-reference (casual):** 나 (na) - casual "I"
- **Self-reference (feminine):** 나 (na) - used by both genders
- **Self-reference (more feminine):** 저는 (jeoneun) - can sound more feminine
- **Referring to others (respectful):** ~께서 (kkeoseo) - respectful particle
- **Respectful action:** ~시 (si) - honorific marker
- **Age-appropriate terms:** Older to younger vs younger to older

### Gender-Specific Dialogue Patterns
- **Male speech:** 남성 다움 (namseong daeum) - masculine speech
  - More direct/less apologetic
  - Less emotional expression
  - Different sentence-ending particles
- **Female speech:** 여성 다움 (yeoseong daeum) - feminine speech
  - More apologetic/softer tone
  - More emotional expression
  - Different polite expressions
- **Neutral speech:** 중립 (jungnip) - neutral speech
- **Masculine endings:** ~다 (da) - more direct
- **Feminine endings:** ~요 (yo) - softer
- **Gender-neutral endings:** ~어요/아요 (eoyo/ayo) - polite and neutral
- **Gender-specific expressions:** Different expressions based on gender
- **Age-appropriate speech:** Language appropriate to character age
- **Social role speech:** Language appropriate to character role

### Contextual Language
- **Professional settings:** 직장 언어 (jikjang eoneo) - workplace language
- **School setting:** 학교 언어 (hakgyo eoneo) - school language
- **Family setting:** 가족 언어 (gajok eoneo) - family language
- **Romantic setting:** 연애 언어 (yeonae eoneo) - dating language
- **Battle setting:** 전투 언어 (jeontu eoneo) - combat language
- **Fantasy setting:** 판타지 언어 (pantaji eoneo) - fantasy language
- **Comedy setting:** 코미디 언어 (komidi eoneo) - comedy language
- **Dramatic setting:** 드라마 언어 (deurama eoneo) - drama language

### Advanced Dialogue Markers
- **But/However:** 하지만 (hajiman) - more formal
- **But/However:** 근데 (geunde) - more casual
- **Well then:** 그럼 (geureom) - well then
- **Really:** 정말 (jeongmal) - really (intensifier)
- **Actually:** 사실 (sasil) - actually
- **By the way:** 그런데 (geureonde) - by the way
- **In that case:** 그렇다면 (geureotamyeon) - if that's the case
- **I mean:** 그러니까 (geureokeunni) - so to speak
- **You know:** 아시다시피 (asidasipsi) - as you know
- **I think:** ~다고 생각해 (dago saenggage) - I think

### Question Formation
- **Formal questions:** ~습니까? (supniga?) - formal question
- **Polite questions:** ~어요/아요? (eoyo/ayo?) - polite question
- **Casual questions:** ~해? (hae?) - casual question
- **Yes/No questions:** ~죠? (jyo?) - expectation question
- **Rhetorical questions:** ~겠죠? (getjyo?) - rhetorical question
- **Confirming questions:** ~지 않아요? (ji anayo?) - confirmation
- **Alternative questions:** ~할까요 아닐까요? (halkkayo anilkka?) - alternative
- **Indirect questions:** ~을지 (eulji) - indirect question
- **Question tags:** ~(으)ㄴ가요? (eungayo?) - question tag
- **Intensive questions:** 도대체 (dodaeche) - on earth (intensive)

### Conditional and Hypothetical Expressions
- **If:** ~다면 (damyeon) - if (condition)
- **If:** ~라면 (ramyeon) - if (condition)
- **If:** ~으면 (eumyeon) - if (condition)
- **Even if:** ~라도 (rado) - even if
- **Even if:** ~이라도 (irado) - even if
- **Despite:** ~에도 (e-unde) - despite
- **As long as:** ~하는 한 (haneun han) - as long as
- **Suppose:** 가정하면 (gajeonghamyeon) - suppose
- **In case:** 경우에 (gyeoe-e) - in case
- **Should:** ~해야 (haeya) - should

### Emotional and Intensity Markers
- **Emphasis:** 정말로 (jeongmallo) - really/seriously
- **Emphasis:** 진짜로 (jinjjaro) - really (casual)
- **Doubt:** 혹시 (hoksi) - maybe/perhaps
- **Certainty:** 확실히 (hwakshilhi) - certainly
- **Surprise:** 어머나 (eomeona) - oh my (surprise)
- **Frustration:** 참 (chan) - really (frustration)
- **Sarcasm:** 어이 (eoi) - really (sarcasm)
- **Intensifier:** 완전 (wanjeon) - completely (casual)
- **Intensifier:** 너무 (neomu) - too much/very
- **Downplay:** 좀 (jom) - somewhat (downplay)

### Advanced Conversation Patterns
- **Agreement:** 맞아요 (majayo) - that's right
- **Disagreement:** 아니에요 (anieyo) - no (polite)
- **Partial agreement:** 그럴 수도 있어요 (geureol sudo issayo) - that could be
- **Disagreement with reason:** 하지만 (hajiman) - but
- **Clarification:** 그러니까 (geureokeunni) - so
- **Change of topic:** 그런데 (geureonde) - but/however
- **Interrupting:** 실례지만 (shillyejiman) - excuse me but
- **Checking understanding:** 이해하셨어요? (ihaehaesdeosseoyo?) - did you understand?
- **Asking for repetition:** 다시 말해 주시겠어요? (dasi malhae jusigesseoyo?) - would you repeat?
- **Confirming:** 맞죠? (mattjiyo?) - right?

### Character Voice Differentiation
- **Arrogant character:** 거만한 말투 (geomanhan maltu) - arrogant tone
- **Shy character:** 수줍은 말투 (sujubeun maltu) - shy tone
- **Confident character:** 자신 있는 말투 (jachim inneun maltu) - confident tone
- **Sarcastic character:** 풍자적인 말투 (pungjajeogin maltu) - sarcastic tone
- **Emotional character:** 감정적인 말투 (gamjeongjeogin maltu) - emotional tone
- **Logical character:** 논리적인 말투 (nonlijeogin maltu) - logical tone
- **Mysterious character:** 수수께끼 같은 말투 (susuggekke tteneun maltu) - riddle-like tone
- **Direct character:** 직설적인 말투 (jiksuljeogin maltu) - direct tone
- **Indirect character:** 간접적인 말투 (ganjeokjeogin maltu) - indirect tone
- **Poetic character:** 시적인 말투 (sijeogin maltu) - poetic tone

### Advanced Sound Effects and Punctuation
- **Ellipsis:** ... - pause/thought
- **Dash:** — - interruption/long pause
- **Emphasis:** **Bold** - Korean doesn't have bold in text, but meaning emphasis
- **Thought bubbles:** 생각 말풍선 (saenggak malpungseon) - thought bubble
- **Inner monologue:** 독백 (dokbaek) - inner monologue
- **Narrator:** 화자 (hwaja) - narrator
- **Voice-over:** 나레이션 (nareiseon) - voice-over
- **Subtitle:** 자막 (jamat) - subtitle/caption
- **Caption box:** 캡션 박스 (kaepson baksu) - caption box
- **Letter/Note:** 편지 (pyeonji) - letter in text
