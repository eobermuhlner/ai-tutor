## Lesson Goals

- Learn vocabulary for daily routine activities in slice of life manhwa
- Understand terms related to school and work life
- Master household and domestic vocabulary used in daily life settings

## Grammar Focus

**Daily Action Constructions:** Using time expressions and routine verbs in daily contexts

**Rule:** Korean daily life expressions often use specific time words and routine action verbs to indicate habitual activities

Examples:
- **아침 (achim)** - morning
- **점심 (jeonbim)** - lunch
- **저녁 (jeonyeok)** - evening/dinner
- **~하다 (hada)** - to do (for daily activities)

This lesson continues our vocabulary-focused approach by emphasizing daily life terms essential for understanding slice of life manhwa.

## Practice Patterns

1. **Routine Recognition:** Identify daily activities from scene descriptions
2. **Context Identification:** Recognize school, work, or home settings
3. **Activity Matching:** Match activities with appropriate vocabulary
4. **Schedule Understanding:** Understand time-related daily activities
5. **Social Activity Recognition:** Identify social interactions in daily life

## Common Mistakes to Watch

- **Cultural Context:** Missing cultural specificities in daily Korean life
- **Formal vs Informal:** Not understanding when to use formal/informal language in daily contexts
- **School Structure:** Confusing Korean school system with other countries
- **Work Culture:** Missing aspects of Korean work culture and hierarchy
- **Daily Routines:** Not understanding typical Korean daily schedules

3. **Family Hierarchy:** The importance of age and family relationships in daily interactions

5. **Social Hierarchy:** How social status affects daily interactions
6. **Living Spaces:** Typical Korean living arrangements and home customs
