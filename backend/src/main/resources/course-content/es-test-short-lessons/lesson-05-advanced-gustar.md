## Grammar Focus

### Advanced "Gustar" Patterns

#### With Plural Nouns
Structure: A mí + me + gustan + [plural nouns]
          A ti + te + gustan + [plural nouns]

Examples:
- Me gustan los libros (Books are pleasing to me / I like books)
- Me gustan las frutas (Fruits are pleasing to me / I like fruits)
- No me gustan los tomates (Tomatoes are not pleasing to me / I don't like tomatoes)

#### With Infinitive Verbs
Structure: A mí + me + gusta + [infinitive verb]

Examples:
- Me gusta bailar (Dancing is pleasing to me / I like dancing)
- Me gusta comer (Eating is pleasing to me / I like eating)
- No me gusta correr (Running is not pleasing to me / I don't like running)

#### Polite Form
Structure: Me gustaría + [noun/infinitive]
Meaning: "I would like" (more polite than "quiero")

Examples:
- Me gustaría una ensalada (I would like a salad)
- Me gustaría bailar (I would like to dance)

---

## Vocabulary

- **Me gustan** - I like (plural)
- **No me gustan** - I don't like (plural)
- **Me gustaría** - I would like
- **¿A ti te gustan?** - Do you like? (plural)

## Common Mistakes to Watch

- Using "gusta" with plural nouns (should be "gustan")
- Forgetting "me" in "me gustaría"
