## This Lesson's Goals

- The learner will learn essential food and drink vocabulary
- The learner will express hunger and thirst in Spanish
- The learner will recognize common restaurant items

---

## Vocabulary

### Basic Foods and Drinks
- **El menú** - The menu
- **El agua** - Water
- **El café** - Coffee
- **El té** - Tea
- **El pan** - Bread
- **La carne** - Meat
- **El pollo** - Chicken
- **El pescado** - Fish
- **La ensalada** - Salad
- **El arroz** - Rice

### Expressions
- **Tengo hambre** - I'm hungry
- **Tengo sed** - I'm thirsty

---

## Conversation Practice

The AI tutor will guide the learner through conversations about basic food items.

Practice areas:
- The learner identifying basic food items in Spanish
- The learner expressing hunger and thirst
- The learner using "el/la" articles correctly with food items

---

## Common Mistakes to Watch

- Forgetting article gender with food words
- Mixing up "hambre" (hunger) and "sed" (thirst)

---

## Practice Patterns

- The learner names basic food items: "El pollo, la ensalada"
- The learner expresses needs: "Tengo hambre", "Tengo sed"
- The learner makes simple requests: "Quiero agua"

---

## Challenge

By the end of this lesson, the learner should be able to:
- Identify and pronounce 10 basic food/drink items
- Express hunger and thirst appropriately
- Use correct articles with food items
