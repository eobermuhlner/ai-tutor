## This Lesson's Goals

- The learner will ask for menu and bill politely
- The learner will use basic restaurant phrases
- The learner will make simple food orders

---

## Vocabulary

- **El menú** - The menu
- **La cuenta** - The bill
- **El camarero/la camarera** - The waiter/waitress
- **La orden** - The order
- **¿Qué recomienda?** - What do you recommend?
- **Por favor** - Please
- **Gracias** - Thank you

---

## Key Phrases

### Getting Service
- Quiero el menú, por favor - I want the menu, please
- ¿Puedo pedir? - Can I order?
- Disculpe - Excuse me (to get attention)

### Ordering
- Quiero... - I want...
- Me gustaría... - I would like...
- ¿Puedo tener...? - Can I have...?

### At the End
- La cuenta, por favor - The bill, please
- ¿La cuenta, por favor? - The check, please

---

## Conversation Practice

The AI tutor will guide the learner through basic restaurant interactions.

Practice areas:
- The learner asking for and receiving a menu
- The learner making simple orders
- The learner requesting the bill

---

## Common Mistakes to Watch

- Forgetting politeness expressions like "por favor"
- Using wrong phrases at the end of the meal

---

## Practice Patterns

- The learner asks for menu: "Quiero el menú, por favor"
- The learner orders food: "Quiero una ensalada y agua"
- The learner requests bill: "La cuenta, por favor"

---

## Challenge

By the end of this lesson, the learner should be able to:
- Ask for menu, order simple items, request bill
- Use polite expressions consistently
- Navigate basic restaurant interaction
