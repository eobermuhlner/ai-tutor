## This Lesson's Goals

- The learner will express dietary restrictions and allergies
- The learner will ask about ingredients in dishes
- The learner will make special requests at restaurants

---

## Vocabulary

### Dietary Restrictions
- **Soy alérgico/a** - I'm allergic
- **Sin sal** - Without salt
- **Sin azúcar** - Without sugar
- **Comida picante** - Spicy food
- **Comida no picante** - Not spicy food
- **Soy vegetariano/a** - I am vegetarian
- **Soy vegano/a** - I am vegan

### Ingredients and Requests
- **¿Lleva...?** - Does it have...?
- **¿Tiene...?** - Does it have...?
- **¿Puedo pedir sin...?** - Can I order without...?
- **¿Hay opciones vegetarianas?** - Are there vegetarian options?

---

## Expressions

### Expressing Restrictions
- Soy alérgico al maní (I'm allergic to peanuts)
- Soy vegetariana (I am vegetarian)
- No como carne (I don't eat meat)

### Asking About Ingredients
- ¿Lleva pollo este plato? (Does this dish have chicken?)
- ¿Tiene leche? (Does it have milk?)
- ¿Qué ingredientes tiene? (What ingredients does it have?)

### Making Requests
- ¿Puedo pedir sin sal? (Can I order without salt?)
- ¿Hay algo sin gluten? (Is there anything without gluten?)

## Conversation Practice

The AI tutor will guide the learner through scenarios involving dietary needs and special requests.

Practice areas:
- The learner stating dietary restrictions
- The learner asking about ingredients in dishes
- The learner making special requests to the server

---

## Common Mistakes to Watch

- Confusing "alérgico" with "no me gusta" (allergy vs. preference)
- Using wrong question words when asking about ingredients

---

## Practice Patterns

- The learner states restrictions: "Soy alérgico al camarón"
- The learner asks about ingredients: "¿Lleva huevos?"
- The learner makes requests: "¿Puedo pedir sin ajo?"

---

## Challenge

By the end of this lesson, the learner should be able to:
- Express dietary restrictions and allergies
- Ask about ingredients in a dish
