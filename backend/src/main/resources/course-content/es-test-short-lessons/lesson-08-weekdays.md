## This Lesson's Goals

- The learner will know the days of the week

## Vocabulary

### Weekdays
- **Lunes** - Monday
- **Martes** - Tuesday
- **Miércoles** - Wednesday
- **Jueves** - Thursday
- **Viernes** - Friday
- **Sábado** - Saturday
- **Domingo** - Sunday

## Challenge

By the end of this lesson, the learner should be able to:
- Express the weekdays
