## This Week's Goals

- Learn vocabulary for daily routine activities in slice of life manga
- Understand terms related to school and work life
- Master household and domestic vocabulary used in daily life settings

## Grammar Focus

**Daily Action Constructions:** Using time expressions and routine verbs in daily contexts

**Rule:** Japanese daily life expressions often use specific time words and routine action verbs to indicate habitual activities

Examples:
- **朝 (asa)** - morning
- **昼 (hiru)** - lunch
- **夕方 (yūgata)** - evening/dinner time
- **〜する (suru)** - to do (for daily activities)

This lesson continues our vocabulary-focused approach by emphasizing daily life terms essential for understanding slice of life manga.
