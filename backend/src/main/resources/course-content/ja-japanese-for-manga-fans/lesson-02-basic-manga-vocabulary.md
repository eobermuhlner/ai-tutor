## Lesson Goals

- Learn vocabulary for different character types in manga
- Understand common locations and settings
- Master terms related to manga storytelling and narrative elements

## Grammar Focus

**Basic Adjective Descriptions:** Using adjectives to describe characteristics

**Rule:** Japanese adjectives can be used to directly modify nouns or come before them in sentences

Examples:
- **可愛い (かわいい)** - cute/lovable
- **怖い (こわい)** - scary/frightening
- **面白い (おもしろい)** - interesting/fun

This lesson continues our focus on vocabulary acquisition for manga reading comprehension.

2. **Setting Identification:** Recognize common manga locations from scene descriptions

4. **Relationship Mapping:** Understand character relationships and romantic dynamics
5. **Physical Description Practice:** Describe characters using appropriate adjectives

## Common Mistakes to Watch

- **Hierarchy Confusion:** Mixing up 'senpai' (senior) and 'kōhai' (junior) relationships which are important in Japanese culture
- **Cultural Context:** Not understanding the significance of family relationships in Japanese stories
- **Formal vs Informal:** Confusing formal and informal expressions used between different character types
- **Time Context:** Misunderstanding temporal terms like "flashback" or "foreshadowing"
- **Genre Expectations:** Not recognizing when certain relationship dynamics are typical of specific genres

## Cultural Notes

1. **Senpai-Kōhai:** The senior-junior relationship is very important in Japanese culture and frequently appears in school and workplace manga
2. **Family Honor:** Family relationships and honor often play crucial roles in Japanese narratives
3. **Social Hierarchy:** Understanding class differences and social positions is important for reading many manga
4. **School Culture:** Japanese schools have specific traditions and social dynamics that appear frequently in school-based manga
5. **Age Terms:** Japanese has different terms for relatives based on age relationships (older brother vs younger brother)
6. **Romance Terms:** Japanese has specific terms for romantic concepts that may not have direct English equivalents
7. **Respect Language:** Characters use different language levels based on social position, which affects word choice and endings
