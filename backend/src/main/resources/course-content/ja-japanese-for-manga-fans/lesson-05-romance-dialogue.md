## Grammar Focus

**Romantic Expression Constructions:** Using keigo (honorifics) and soft language in romantic contexts

**Rule:** Japanese romantic expressions often use soft, gentle language with specific keigo forms to convey affection and respect

Examples:
- **愛してる (あいしてる)** - I love you (casual)
- **愛しています (あいしています)** - I love you (polite form)
- **好きです (すきです)** - I like you (polite)
- **大好き (だいすき)** - I love you very much (casual)

This lesson continues our focus on vocabulary while introducing important romantic expressions used frequently in manga.

## Vocabulary

### Love Expressions
- **愛 (あい)** - love
- **愛してる (あいしてる)** - I love you (informal)
- **愛しています (あいしています)** - I love you (formal)
- **恋に落ちる (こいにおちる)** - to fall in love
- **恋人 (こいびと)** - lover/romantic partner
- **恋愛 (れんあい)** - romance/love affair
- **付き合っている (つきあっている)** - to date/go out with
- **恋心 (こいごころ)** - affection/love
- **想い (おもい)** - affection/love (Japanese concept)

### Romantic Feelings
- ** Crush ** - 気になる (きになる) - to have a crush on someone
- **片思い (かたおもい)** - one-sided love/unrequited love
- **恋心 (こいごころ)** - having a crush on someone
- **心 (こころ)** - heart/feeling (formal)
- **心をくれる (こころをくれる)** - to give one's heart
- **心を掴む (こころをつかむ)** - to win someone's heart
- **心を盗む (こころをぬすむ)** - to steal someone's heart
- **心臓がドキドキ (しんぞうがどきどき)** - heart is beating (for someone)

### Confession Terms
- **告白 (kokuhaku)** - confession/declaration of love
- **愛の告白 (ai no kokuhaku)** - love confession
- **告白する (kokuhaku suru)** - to confess
- **プロポーズ (puropōzu)** - marriage proposal
- **告白の場所 (kokuhaku no basho)** - confession spot
- **告白の機会 (kokuhaku no kikai)** - opportunity for confession
- **勇気 (yūki)** - courage (to confess)
- **勇気を出す (yūki o dasu)** - to gather courage
- **本心 (honshin)** - sincere/honest feelings

### Romantic Actions
- **キス (kisu)** - kiss (from English "kiss")
- **おでこにキス (odeko ni kisu)** - forehead kiss
- **咲く (saku)** - to bloom (figuratively, for feelings)
- **囁く (sasayaku)** - to whisper
- **耳元で囁く (mimimoto de sasayaku)** - to whisper in ear
- **手をつなぐ (te o tsunagu)** - to hold hands
- **太ももに触れる (futomomo ni fureru)** - to touch thigh (intimate)
- **抱きしめる (dakishimeru)** - to embrace/hug
- **ぎゅっと抱きしめる (gyutto dakishimeru)** - to hug tightly
- **包み込む (tsutsumikomu)** - to wrap/cuddle

### Romantic Scenes and Situations
- **デート (dēto)** - date (from English "date")
- **ロマンス (romansu)** - romance
- **ロマンチック (romanchikku)** - romantic (from English)
- **すれ違い (surechigai)** - romantic push and pull
- **恋の駆け引き (koi no kakehiki)** - in the middle of a crush/romance
- **片想い中 (kataomoi chū)** - in the middle of a crush/romance
- **恋の相手 (koi no aite)** - crush person
- **恋愛関係 (ren'ai kankei)** - love line (romantic subplot)
- **三角関係 (sankaku kankei)** - love triangle
- **恋愛物語 (ren'ai monogatari)** - love story

### Romantic Feelings and Emotions
- **ときめき (ときめき)** - flutter/anticipation (unique Japanese emotion)
- **高鳴る (takanaru)** - trembling/excitement
- **胸が高鳴る (mune ga takanaru)** - heart flutters
- **心臓がドキドキ (shinzō ga dokidoki)** - heart pounds
- **心臓がドクドク (shinzō ga dokudoku)** - heart beats fast
- **ドキドキ (dokidoki)** - heart beats fast (onomatopoeia)
- **胸がときめく (mune ga tokimeku)** - heart is fluttering
- **顔が赤くなる (kao ga akaku naru)** - face turns red/blushes
- **頬が熱くなる (hoho ga atsuku naru)** - cheeks are hot
- **めまい (memai)** - dizzy/overwhelmed (with emotion)

### Romantic Dialogue Phrases
- **私も (watashi mo)** - me too (I feel the same way)
- **私もそうです (watashi mo sō desu)** - I feel that way too
- **私も好きです (watashi mo suki desu)** - I like you too
- **私も愛してる (watashi mo aishiteru)** - I love you too
- **私も待ってました (watashi mo matte mashita)** - I've been waiting for this too
- **私も待ってください (watashi mo matte kudasai)** - I'm waiting too (for a response)
- **待つ (matsu)** - to wait (for you)
- **待っててください (matte te kudasai)** - I'll wait (for you)
- **一緒に (issho ni)** - together
- **永遠に (eien ni)** - forever

### Intimate Expressions
- **君を愛してる (kimi o aishiteru)** - I love you (you)
- **私の人 (watashi no hito)** - my person
- **私の恋 (watashi no koi)** - my love
- **俺の彼女 (ore no kanojo)** - my person
- **甘い (amai)** - sweet
- **甘々 (amaama)** - sweetly (romance scene slang)
- **甘くする (amaku suru)** - to make sweet
- **甘くする (amaku suru)** - sweetly
- **甘くする (amaku suru)** - to make sweet

### Romantic Rejection and Heartbreak
- **断る (kotowaru)** - rejection
- **断る (kotowaru)** - to reject
- **断られた (kotowarareta)** - to be rejected
- **傷 (kizu)** - wound/heartbreak
- **失恋 (shitsuren)** - heartbreak/rejection
- **片思いの終わり (kataomoi no owari)** - end of unrequited love
- **嫌い (kirai)** - hate/don't like
- **嫌いではない (kirai dewa nai)** - don't dislike (positive way of saying you like)
- **気持ちがない (kimochi ga nai)** - don't have feelings for you
- **心をくれません (kokoro o kuremasen)** - can't give my heart

2. **Indirect Communication:** Japanese romantic expressions often use indirect language
3. **Age Hierarchy:** Romantic expressions change based on age relationships
4. **Physical Boundaries:** What is considered acceptable physical intimacy in Japanese culture
5. **Confession Culture:** The importance of formal confessions ("kokuhaku") in Japanese romance
6. **Romantic Timing:** When it's appropriate to express romantic feelings in Japanese culture
7. **Heart Symbol:** The cultural significance of heart imagery and fluttering feelings in Japanese romance
