## Lesson Goals

- Learn advanced dialogue structures used in manga
- Understand character-specific speech patterns and voice
- Master formal and informal language usage in different contexts

## Grammar Focus

**Advanced Dialogue Constructions:** Using keigo (honorifics) and character-specific expressions

**Rule:** Japanese dialogue varies significantly based on social hierarchy, age, gender, and character archetypes. Different characters use distinct speech patterns that reflect their personality and status.

Examples:
- **Formal:** 〜です/ます (desu/masu) ending - polite, respectful
- **Polite ending:** 〜ございます (gozaimasu) - very polite ending
- **Casual ending:** 〜よ (yo) - casual, familiar
- **Gender-specific:** Different expressions used by male/female characters
- **Age hierarchy:** Language changes based on speaker/addressee age relationship

This lesson combines our previous vocabulary knowledge with advanced dialogue patterns used in Japanese manga.

## Vocabulary

### Formal vs Informal Language
- **Formal ending:** です/ます (です/ます) - most polite ending
- **Very polite ending:** 〜でございます (〜でございます) - very polite
- **Casual ending:** 〜よ (〜よ) - informal with friends/equal status
- **Honorific language:** 敬語 (けいご) - respectful language
- **Humble language:** 謙譲語 (けんじょうご) - humble language about oneself
- **Intimate language:** 親密語 (しんみつご) - informal language (close relationships)
- **Respectful verb forms:** 〜れる/〜られる (〜れる/〜られる) - honorific marker
- **Formal verb endings:** 〜ます形 (〜ますがた) - written/formal speech
- **Casual speech:** 〜する → 〜す - spoken informal
- **Contraction patterns:** Formal to informal contractions

### Character-Specific Speech Patterns
- **ツンデレ (ツンデレ)** - 愛をうまく表現できない性格 (あいをうまくひょうげんできないせいかく) - hard to show warmth personality
- **クーデレ (クーデレ)** - 冷血な性格 (れいけつなせいかく) - cold-blooded personality
- **ダンデレ (ダンデレ)** - 静かで内向的な性格 (しずかでないこうてきなせいかく) - quiet and introverted personality
- **カミデレ (Kamidere)** - 自信がある性格 (jishin ga aru seikaku) - confident personality
- **ヤンデレ (Yandere)** - 愛に没頭する性格 (ai ni bottō suru seikaku) - overly devoted personality
- **Stoic male:** 沈黙寡言な性格 (chinmoku kagen na seikaku) - few-word personality
- **Tsundere female:** 愛を隠す性格 (ai o kakusu seikaku) - warmth-hiding personality
- **Cheerful character:** 明るく前向きな性格 (akaruku maemuki na seikaku) - bright and positive personality
- **Serious character:** 真面目な性格 (majime na seikaku) - serious personality
- **Playful character:** 淘気な性格 (tōki na seikaku) - mischievous personality

### Formal Address Terms
- **You (polite):** 〜さん (san) - respectful suffix
- **You (very formal):** ご自身 (gojishin) - honorific "you"
- **Self-reference (humble):** 私 (watashi) - humble "I"
- **Self-reference (formal):** 私は (watashi wa) - formal "I am"
- **Self-reference (casual):** 僕 (boku)/俺 (ore) - casual "I"
- **Self-reference (more feminine):** 私 (watashi) - more feminine
- **Referring to others (respectful):** 〜様 (sama) - respectful particle
- **Respectful action:** 〜れる (rareru) - honorific marker
- **Age-appropriate terms:** Older to younger vs younger to older

### Gender-Specific Dialogue Patterns
- **Male speech:** 男性的 (danseiteki) - masculine speech
  - More direct/less apologetic
  - Less emotional expression
  - Different sentence-ending particles
- **Female speech:** 女性的 (joseiteki) - feminine speech
  - More apologetic/softer tone
  - More emotional expression
  - Different polite expressions
- **Neutral speech:** 中立 (chūritsu) - neutral speech
- **Masculine endings:** 〜よ (yo) - more direct
- **Feminine endings:** 〜わ (wa) - softer
- **Gender-neutral endings:** です/ます (desu/masu) - polite and neutral
- **Gender-specific expressions:** Different expressions based on gender
- **Age-appropriate speech:** Language appropriate to character age
- **Social role speech:** Language appropriate to character role

### Contextual Language
- **Professional settings:** 職場言語 (shokuba gengo) - workplace language
- **School setting:** 学校言語 (gakkō gengo) - school language
- **Family setting:** 家族言語 (kazoku gengo) - family language
- **Romantic setting:** 恋愛言語 (ren'ai gengo) - dating language
- **Battle setting:** 戦闘言語 (sentō gengo) - combat language
- **Fantasy setting:** ファンタジー言語 (fantajī gengo) - fantasy language
- **Comedy setting:** コメディ言語 (komedi gengo) - comedy language
- **Dramatic setting:** ドラマ言語 (dorama gengo) - drama language

### Advanced Dialogue Markers
- **But/However:** しかし (shikashi) - more formal
- **But/However:** でも (demo) - more casual
- **Well then:** それでは (sore dewa) - well then
- **Really:** 本当に (hontō ni) - really (intensifier)
- **Actually:** 実は (jitsu wa) - actually
- **By the way:** ところで (tokoro de) - by the way
- **In that case:** それでは (sore dewa) - if that's the case
- **I mean:** つまり (tsumari) - so to speak
- **You know:** ご存じの通り (gozonji no tōri) - as you know
- **I think:** 〜と思います (to omoimasu) - I think

### Question Formation
- **Formal questions:** 〜でしょうか (deshou ka) - formal question
- **Polite questions:** ますか (masu ka) - polite question
- **Casual questions:** 〜? (ka) - casual question
- **Yes/No questions:** 〜でしょうか (deshou ka) - expectation question
- **Rhetorical questions:** 〜のですか (no desu ka) - rhetorical question
- **Confirming questions:** 〜ですね (desu ne) - confirmation
- **Alternative questions:** 〜かそれとも (ka soretomo) - alternative
- **Indirect questions:** 〜かどうか (ka dōka) - indirect question
- **Question tags:** 〜でしょう (deshō) - question tag
- **Intensive questions:** 一体 (ittai) - on earth (intensive)

### Emotional and Intensity Markers
- **Emphasis:** やはり (yappari) - really/seriously
- **Emphasis:** 本当に (hontō ni) - really (casual)
- **Doubt:** ひょっとして (hyottoshite) - maybe/perhaps
- **Certainty:** 確かに (tashika ni) - certainly
- **Surprise:** 何てこと (nante koto) - oh my (surprise)
- **Frustration:** まったく (mattaku) - really (frustration)
- **Sarcasm:** まあ (mā) - really (sarcasm)
- **Intensifier:** すごく (sugoku) - completely (casual)
- **Intensifier:** とても (totemo) - too much/very
- **Downplay:** ちょっと (chotto) - somewhat (downplay)

### Advanced Conversation Patterns
- **Agreement:** その通りです (sono tōri desu) - that's right
- **Disagreement:** いいえ (iie) - no (polite)
- **Partial agreement:** ある程度 (aru teido) - that could be
- **Disagreement with reason:** しかし (shikashi) - but
- **Clarification:** つまり (tsumari) - so
- **Change of topic:** ところで (tokoro de) - but/however
- **Interrupting:** 失礼ですが (shitsurei desu ga) - excuse me but
- **Checking understanding:** 理解しましたか (rikai shimashita ka) - did you understand?
- **Asking for repetition:** もう一度お願いします (mō ichido onegaishimasu) - would you repeat?
- **Confirming:** ですね (desu ne) - right?

- **Confident character:** 自信のある話し方 (jishin no aru hanashikata) - confident tone
- **Sarcastic character:** 風刺的な話し方 (fūshiteki na hanashikata) - sarcastic tone
- **Emotional character:** 感情的な話し方 (kanjōteki na hanashikata) - emotional tone
- **Logical character:** 論理的な話し方 (ronriteki na hanashikata) - logical tone
- **Mysterious character:** 謎めいた話し方 (nazomeita hanashikata) - riddle-like tone
- **Direct character:** 直接的な話し方 (chokusetsuteki na hanashikata) - direct tone
- **Indirect character:** 間接的な話し方 (kansetsuteki na hanashikata) - indirect tone
- **Poetic character:** 詩的な話し方 (shiteki na hanashikata) - poetic tone
