## Lesson Goals

- Learn vocabulary for fantasy creatures and beings in manga
- Understand terms related to magic and supernatural powers
- Master world-building terminology used in fantasy settings

## Grammar Focus

**Fantasy Description Constructions:** Using descriptive adjectives and compound nouns in fantasy contexts

**Rule:** Japanese fantasy terms often use compound nouns and descriptive adjectives to create complex fantasy concepts

Examples:
- **魔法使い (まほうつかい)** - magician/sorcerer (magic-user)
- **ドラゴン (どらごん)** - dragon (from English "dragon")
- **魔法 (まほう)** - magic (magic-power)

This lesson focuses on fantasy vocabulary essential for understanding the popular fantasy genre in Japanese manga.
