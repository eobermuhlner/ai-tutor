## Lesson Goals

- Learn vocabulary for action verbs and movements in manga
- Understand terms related to combat and fighting scenes
- Master expressions for speed, impact, and intensity in action scenes

## Grammar Focus

**Action Verb Conjugations:** Understanding present and past tense for action verbs

**Rule:** Japanese action verbs often change form depending on the intensity or completion of the action, especially in action scenes

Examples:
- **飛ぶ (とぶ)** - to fly (present)
- **舞い上がる (まいあがる)** - to soar upward (intensified form)
- **追う (おう)** - to chase
- **追いかける (おいかける)** - to chase after/run after

This lesson continues our vocabulary-focused approach by emphasizing action-related terms essential for understanding dynamic scenes in manga.

## Vocabulary

- **歩く (あるく)** - to walk
- **走る (はしる)** - to run
- **跳ぶ (とぶ)** - to jump
- **座る (すわる)** - to sit
- **立つ (たつ)** - to stand
- **寝る (ねる)** - to lie down
- **屈む (かがむ)** - to kneel/bend
- **よろめく (よろめく)** - to stagger/wobble
- **よろける (よろける)** - to stumble
- **這う (はう)** - to crawl

### Advanced Movement
- **飛ぶ (とぶ)** - to fly
- **薙ぐ (なぐ)** - to sweep/swipe
- **回転する (かいてんする)** - to whirl/swirl
- **巻き付ける (まきつける)** - to wrap around
- **伸ばす (のばす)** - to stretch out
- **巻く (まく)** - to coil/wrap
- **突き出す (つきだす)** - to stretch out/flare out
- **曲げる (まげる)** - to bend/break
- **ぶつかる (ぶつかる)** - to collide/hit against
- **跳ねる (はねる)** - to bounce/kick off

### Combat and Fighting
- **戦う (たたかう)** - to fight
- **戦い (たたかい)** - fight
- **突く (つく)** - to peck/bite
- **打つ (うつ)** - to hit/strike
- **もがく (もがく)** - to struggle/fight
- **格闘 (かくとう)** - combat/fighting
- **撃破 (げきは)** - to break through/defeat
- **攻撃 (こうげき)** - attack
- **防御 (ぼうぎょ)** - defense
- **反撃 (はんげき)** - counterattack

### Combat Actions
- **パンチ (panchi)** - punch (from English "punch")
- **キック (kikku)** - kick (from English "kick")
- **刺す (sasu)** - to stab/pierce
- **突く (tsuku)** - to thrust/kick out
- **薙ぐ (nagu)** - to sweep/swipe
- **伸ばす (nobasu)** - to extend/reach out (in fighting)
- **払い返す (harai kaesu)** - to parry and counter
- **防ぐ (fusegu)** - to block
- **かわす (kawasu)** - to dodge/evade
- **転がる (korogaru)** - to roll/dodge

### Weapon-Based Combat
- **包丁 (hōchō)** - knife/sword
- **武器 (buki)** - weapon
- **剣 (ken)** - sword
- **槍 (yari)** - spear
- **斧 (ono)** - axe
- **弓 (yumi)** - bow
- **矢 (ya)** - arrow
- **銃 (jū)** - gun
- **短刀 (tantō)** - dagger
- **お守り (omamori)** - charm/amulet

### Impact and Force
- **ドッ (dottsu)** - sound of impact
- **パッ (pattsu)** - sound of hitting
- **パキパキ (pakipaki)** - sound of multiple hits
- **ドッカーン (dokkān)** - sound of breaking/crashing
- **シュア (shua)** - sound of slicing
- **キッ (kittsu)** - sound of cutting
- **ドンドン (don don)** - repeated heavy impact
- **パクパク (pakupaku)** - repeated hitting sounds
- **ガシャン (gashan)** - sound of shattering

### Speed and Intensity
- **速く (hayaku)** - quickly/rapidly
- **ゆっくり (yukkuri)** - slowly/cautiously
- **迅速に (shinso ni)** - promptly/swiftly
- **即座に (sokuzo ni)** - immediately
- **一瞬で (isshun de)** - in an instant
- **秀逸 (shūitsu)** - excellence/skill
- **神技 (shingi)** - skillful/remarkable
- **練達 (rendatsu)** - skillful/proficient
- **華麗 (karei)** - splendid/brilliant
- **華麗に (karei ni)** - brilliantly/splendidly

### Special Movement Effects
- **ひらり (hirari)** - flash/suddenly appear
- **するり (sururi)** - swish/swift motion
- **すっと (sutto)** - sudden quick movement
- **ふらりふらり (furari furari)** - rapid back and forth motion
- **シャア (shā)** - slicing sounds
- **すべり (suberi)** - smooth sliding sound
- **ザアー (zā)** - dramatic impact sound
- **ひゅる (hyuru)** - swift movement sound
- **さらり (sarari)** - flowing sound
- **さっくり (sakkuri)** - clean slicing sound

## Conversation Scenarios

As this course focuses on manga reading comprehension, the AI tutor will present descriptions of action scenes and you will identify the types of movements, fighting techniques, and impact levels described. You'll practice recognizing action vocabulary in context.

Scenarios to practice:
- Identifying movement types in action sequences

- Recognizing impact and intensity levels in action scenes

## Practice Patterns

1. **Movement Recognition:** Identify different types of movement from action descriptions
2. **Combat Identification:** Recognize fighting techniques and combat styles
3. **Sound Effect Matching:** Match sound effects to appropriate actions
4. **Intensity Assessment:** Identify how intensity is conveyed in action scenes
5. **Speed Description:** Understand how speed is described in manga

## Common Mistakes to Watch

- **Sound Effect Confusion:** Not understanding the difference between similar onomatopoeia
- **Action Context:** Misunderstanding the context of actions without visual cues
- **Cultural References:** Missing cultural references in fighting styles or techniques
- **Intensity Levels:** Not recognizing when actions are exaggerated for effect
- **Technical Terms:** Confusing similar terms for different types of movements

## Cultural Notes

1. **Martial Arts (武道):** Japanese martial arts often feature in manga, with specific terminology for techniques
2. **Weapon Culture:** Traditional Japanese weapons and their uses in historical manga
3. **Speed Perception:** Japanese culture's appreciation for quick, decisive action in combat
4. **Chivalry:** Honor codes that may appear in certain combat scenes
5. **Training Concepts:** The importance of training and skill development in action manga
6. **Sound Effects:** How sound effects in Japanese manga differ from those in other cultures
7. **Impact Expression:** The cultural significance of showing impact and power in visual storytelling
