## Lesson Goals

- Understand different manga genres and types
- Learn essential vocabulary for describing characters and panels
- Get familiar with common manga expressions and formatting

## Grammar Focus

**Simple Noun Identification:** Use of basic nouns with context clues

**Rule:** Japanese nouns can be identified by their meaning in context, often followed by particles that indicate their role in the sentence.

Examples:
- **漫画 (まんが)** - comic/manga
- **アニメ (あにめ)** - animation (from English "animation")
- **イラスト (いらすと)** - illustration

This course will focus on vocabulary acquisition and context recognition rather than extensive grammar study, as these skills are most important for reading manga.

## Vocabulary

### Manga Genres
- **ロマンス (ろまんす)** - romance
- **アクション (あくしょん)** - action
- **ファンタジー (ふぁんたじー)** - fantasy
- **ドラマ (どらま)** - drama
- **学園 (がくえん)** - school/campus
- **スリラー (すりらー)** - thriller
- **コメディ (こめでぃ)** - comedy
- **武道 (ぶどう)** - martial arts
- **時代 (じだい)** - historical
- **BL (びいえる)** - Boys Love (same-sex romance between males)
- **GL (じいえる)** - Girls Love (same-sex romance between females)
- **slice of life** - 日常 (にちじょう) - daily life

### Character Expressions
- **表情 (ひょうじょう)** - expression
- **微笑 (びしょう)** - smile
- **笑い (わらい)** - laugh/laughter
- **涙 (なみだ)** - tears
- **怒り (いかり)** - anger/rage
- **悲しみ (かなしみ)** - sadness
- **驚き (おどろき)** - surprise
- **失神 (しっしん)** - fainting
- **心臓がドキドキ (しんぞうがどきどき)** - heart beating loudly
- **赤面 (せきめん)** - blush (redness on cheeks)

### Panel and Format Terms
- **パネル (ぱねる)** - panel
- **漫画本 (まんがぼん)** - comic book
- **画像 (がぞう)** - image
- **吹き出し (ふきだし)** - speech bubble
- **思考吹き出し (しこうふきだし)** - thought bubble
- **背景 (はいけい)** - background
- **キャプション (きゃぷしょん)** - caption/text box
- **エフェクト (えふぇくと)** - effect
- **線 (せん)** - line
- **色 (いろ)** - color
- **白黒 (しろくろ)** - black and white
- **カラー (からー)** - color (from English "color")

### Character Descriptions
- **主人公 (しゅじんこう)** - main character/protagonist
- **悪党 (あくとう)** - villain/bad guy
- **サポート (さぽーと)** - supporter/ally
- **ヒロイン (ひろいん)** - heroine
- **男の主人公 (おとこのしゅじんこう)** - male protagonist
- **女の主人公 (おんなのしゅじんこう)** - female protagonist
- **準主役 (じゅんしゅやく)** - supporting character
- **端役 (はしやく)** - minor character
- **外見 (がいけん)** - appearance/looks
- **性格 (せいかく)** - personality

## Conversation Scenarios

As this course focuses on reading manga rather than daily conversation, the AI tutor will help you understand and identify vocabulary used in different manga scenes and contexts. You will practice recognizing terms for different genres, character expressions, and panel compositions.

Scenarios to practice:
- Identifying genre and mood from panel descriptions
- Understanding character expressions and emotions
- Recognizing common manga formatting elements

AI tutor will present descriptions of manga scenes and you will identify relevant vocabulary and concepts.

## Practice Patterns

1. **Genre Recognition:** Look at manga covers and identify the genre based on visual cues and vocabulary
2. **Expression Identification:** Study character faces and match expressions with Japanese terms
3. **Format Terms Practice:** Learn to identify different panel types and formatting elements
4. **Character Identification:** Distinguish between protagonist, antagonist, and supporting characters
5. **Vocabulary Matching:** Match Japanese terms with their English equivalents

## Common Mistakes to Watch

- **Reading Direction Confusion:** Manga read right to left, top to bottom (unlike Western comics)
- **Slang vs Formal:** Understanding when informal expressions are used in dialogue
- **Onomatopoeia:** Recognizing sound effects which often use kana or creative spelling
- **Character vs Narration:** Distinguishing between character dialogue and narrator text
- **Cultural Context:** Understanding Japanese-specific cultural references that may not be familiar to non-Japanese readers

## Cultural Notes

1. **Manga vs Anime:** Japanese manga developed separately from anime, with different artistic and storytelling traditions
2. **Tankōbon Format:** Serialized manga chapters are later collected in bound volumes called tankōbon
3. **Genre Preferences:** BL (Boys Love) and GL (Girls Love) have significant audiences in Japan and are mainstream genres
4. **Artistic Styles:** Japanese manga features distinctive art styles that differ from Korean manhwa and Western comics
5. **Platform Culture:** Many popular manga start in weekly magazines like Shōnen Jump or Monthly Gangan
6. **Age Ratings:** Japanese manga have clear target demographics (shōnen, shōjo, seinen, josei) that indicate appropriate audiences
7. **Episode Structure:** Manga chapters typically have consistent numbering and are released weekly or monthly
